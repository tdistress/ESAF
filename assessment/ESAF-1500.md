# ESAF-1500 Assessment Guide

**Status:** Working Draft
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Purpose

ESAF-1500 defines the shared evidence, assessment-result, and maturity
semantics used by ESAF assessments, crosswalks, and profiles.

## Scope

This guide applies to assessments of ESAF requirements, controls, capabilities,
profiles, and mapped outcomes. It does not define a certification scheme,
automate professional judgment, or establish external-framework compliance.

## Relationship to the ESAF library

ESAF-1000 establishes the enterprise AI standard. ESAF-1100 remains
authoritative for control requirements, assessment procedures, assessment
methods, determinations, and definitions of design and operating effectiveness.
ESAF-1500 is authoritative for shared assessment records and maturity
semantics. ESAF-1600 shall reuse these records when documenting crosswalk
evidence, and ESAF-1800 profiles shall reuse these semantics rather than define
profile-local substitutes.

## Assessment principles

An assessment shall state its purpose, scope, boundary, period, methods,
evidence, limitations, and traceability. An assessor shall exercise
professional judgment and record an independence statement appropriate to the
assessment purpose. Assessment records shall distinguish observed evidence from
inference, and shall preserve known limitations rather than conceal them.

An assessment is valid only for its recorded scope and time boundary. A draft
record may document incomplete work, but shall not be represented as final,
approved, or sufficient for a conformance claim.

## Evidence records

Every evidence record shall use the shared contract below. Machine values shall
be recorded in backticks; labels and explanations are human-readable.

| Required field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `$schema` | Repository-relative schema locator. |
| `schema_version` | `0.1.0`. |
| `evidence_id` | Stable identifier matching `^EVD-[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9-]*$`. |
| `title` | Concise human-readable name. |
| `description` | Fact or condition the artifact is intended to support. |
| `evidence_type` | Closed artifact class: `policy`, `procedure`, `record`, `configuration`, `log`, `technical_test`, `observation`, `interview`, `metric`, `contract`, `external_assurance`, or `other`. |
| `source` | Source-system or originating-party identity and source locator. |
| `custodian` | Accountable evidence owner. |
| `collected_at` | RFC 3339 date-time. |
| `collection_method` | How the evidence was obtained. |
| `scope` | In-scope capability, system, control, requirement, or population. |
| `period` | Point-in-time date or inclusive start and end dates. |
| `population` | Defined population and, when sampled, sample and selection rationale. |
| `quality` | Seven attribute evaluations and an overall sufficiency decision. |
| `integrity` | Digest algorithm and lowercase hexadecimal digest, or a documented protected-record locator and verification method. |
| `limitations` | Non-empty known limitations, or an explicit empty array. |
| `retention` | Retention authority and end condition. |
| `traceability` | Stable references to the assessed requirement, procedure, and result that consume the evidence. |

When `evidence_type` is `other`, the record shall include a specific type
description. Evidence identifiers shall be stable and shall not be reused for a
different artifact.

## Evidence quality

Each evidence record shall evaluate all seven attributes using the following
vocabulary.

| Attribute | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **relevance:** | The evidence bears directly on the assessed use, requirement, scope, and period. |
| **reliability:** | The source and collection method support confidence that the evidence is dependable. |
| **completeness:** | The evidence covers the defined population, sample, and facts needed for the stated use. |
| **timeliness:** | The evidence is current enough for the recorded assessment period and decision. |
| **attribution:** | The evidence identifies the originating person, role, system, or accountable party. |
| **integrity:** | The evidence is protected from unauthorized alteration and can be verified. |
| **traceability:** | The evidence is linked to the assessed requirement, procedure, result, and any finding it supports. |

Each attribute evaluation shall contain a `rating` of `adequate`, `limited`,
`inadequate`, or `not_evaluated`, and a non-empty rationale tied to the
assessed use. The `quality` object shall also contain a `sufficiency` value of
`sufficient`, `limited`, or `insufficient`; `evaluated_by`; `evaluated_at` as
an RFC 3339 date-time; and a non-empty `sufficiency_rationale`.

Sufficiency shall be explicit and shall not be calculated by averaging
attribute ratings. `limited`, `inadequate`, and `not_evaluated` attributes
shall remain visible even when the evaluator concludes that evidence is
`sufficient` for a narrowly stated use.

## Assessment scope and methods

An assessment result shall identify the assessed capability or system,
requirements, boundary, exclusions, population, sample, assessment start,
assessment end, and result date. ESAF-1100 methods and determination meanings
shall be reused without modification. The authorized methods are `Examine`,
`Interview`, `Test`, and `Observe`; each method record shall identify its
procedure, object, expected result, and actual work performed.

The determination vocabulary is `satisfied`, `partially_satisfied`,
`not_satisfied`, `not_applicable`, and `not_assessed`. A `not_applicable`
determination shall include an approved applicability rationale and approver. A
`not_assessed` determination shall include a scope-exclusion rationale.

## Assessment results

Every assessment result shall use the shared contract below.

| Required field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `$schema` | Repository-relative schema locator. |
| `schema_version` | `0.1.0`. |
| `result_id` | Stable identifier matching `^ASR-[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9-]*$`. |
| `status` | `draft` or `final`. |
| `title` | Human-readable result title. |
| `assessor` | Identity, role, organization, and independence statement. |
| `assessment_scope` | Assessed capability or system, requirements, boundary, exclusions, population, and sample. |
| `methods` | One or more method records, except for `draft` results and justified final `not_assessed` results. |
| `time_boundary` | Assessment start, assessment end, and result date. |
| `evidence_refs` | One or more stable evidence identifiers, except for `draft` results and justified final `not_assessed` results. |
| `determination` | One ESAF-1100 determination value. |
| `determination_rationale` | Explanation of what the available evidence supports. |
| `design_effectiveness` | `effective`, `partially_effective`, `ineffective`, `not_applicable`, or `not_assessed`. |
| `operating_effectiveness` | `effective`, `partially_effective`, `ineffective`, `not_applicable`, or `not_assessed`. |
| `findings` | Finding records with stable identifiers, or an explicit empty array. |
| `limitations` | Explicit limitations, or an explicit empty array. |
| `traceability` | Assessed ESAF control or requirement identifiers and applicable external or profile identifiers. |
| `change_history` | Version, date, author, and change description. |

A `final` result shall prohibit unresolved placeholder language; require at
least one method and evidence reference except where `not_assessed` is
justified; and require every included finding to have a valid disposition
state.

## Findings and dispositions

Findings are nested within an assessment result and shall use the following
contract.

| Required field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `finding_id` | Stable identifier matching `^FND-[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9-]*$`. |
| `severity` | `critical`, `important`, or `minor`. |
| `status` | `open`, `resolved`, or `accepted`. |
| `affected_requirement_ids` | Identifiers of affected requirements. |
| `statement` | Description of the observed condition. |
| `evidence_refs` | Supporting evidence identifiers. |
| `owner` | Accountable response owner. |
| `disposition` | Governed disposition record. |
| `identified_at` | RFC 3339 date-time. |
| `disposition_at` | RFC 3339 date-time required when status is `resolved` or `accepted`. |

An accepted finding shall also include an acceptance authority, rationale,
expiry or review date, and residual-risk statement. Acceptance records a
governed disposition; it shall not change the underlying determination or
establish control satisfaction.

## Maturity model

The ESAF maturity scale is fixed. The levels are cumulative: a claim at a
level requires every prerequisite level and criterion below it to be met.

| Level | Name | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `M0` | Ad hoc | The capability is absent, materially incomplete, unknown, or performed inconsistently without dependable management. |
| `M1` | Managed | The capability has accountable ownership, planned execution, and repeatable basic operation within the stated scope. |
| `M2` | Defined | The capability uses approved, standardized, documented practices integrated with applicable lifecycle and governance processes. |
| `M3` | Measured | The capability is measured against defined outcomes and thresholds using trustworthy evidence, with exceptions and trends governed. |
| `M4` | Adaptive | The capability uses measured results, change signals, incidents, and lessons to improve predictably while preserving control and evidence integrity. |

A maturity assessment shall contain `$schema`; `schema_version` of `0.1.0`;
`maturity_id` matching `^MAT-[A-Z0-9][A-Z0-9-]*$`; `status` of `draft` or
`final`; exact `scope` and exclusions; `assessor` identity, role,
organization, and independence statement; `assessed_at`; `level`; `criteria`;
`basis_refs`; `limitations`; optional `component_results`; and
`change_history`.

The `criteria` array shall include one entry for every level from `M0` through
the claimed level, in ascending order. Each entry shall include its level,
criterion identifier, statement, `met` boolean, rationale, and basis
references. A final claim above `M0` is invalid when any prerequisite level is
missing, any prerequisite criterion is not met, a required basis reference is
missing or unresolved, or the claim exceeds the lowest substantiated applicable
component result. An organization may define more demanding criteria or
dimensions, but shall not rename the levels, weaken cumulative prerequisites,
infer an unassessed level, or publish a profile-local replacement scale as ESAF
maturity.

## Conformance and maturity

Control conformance and maturity are independent axes. A determination states
what available evidence supports for an assessed requirement, scope, and
period. A maturity level states the substantiated institutionalization of a
capability within its declared scope. Neither result supplies missing evidence
for the other.

No maturity level shall convert `not_assessed`, `not_satisfied`, or
`partially_satisfied` into `satisfied`; establish compliance, certification,
equivalence, endorsement, or external approval; establish evidence sufficiency
outside the recorded scope and period; or imply continuous assurance from
point-in-time evidence.

## Aggregation

Numeric averaging of ordinal maturity levels is prohibited. A roll-up shall
use the lowest substantiated applicable component level and disclose excluded
or not-assessed components. An organization may publish distributions and
counts, but shall not convert them into an unsupported composite score.

## Traceability and change control

Assessment records shall preserve stable references from an assessed ESAF
requirement or control through assessment procedure, evidence, result,
finding, and maturity claim where applicable. Each record shall retain change
history sufficient to identify its version, date, author, and change
description. A changed record shall not silently alter the scope, period,
determination, evidence basis, or maturity claim of an earlier version.

## Implementation and validation

The machine-readable contracts are published at
[assessment/schema/evidence-record.schema.json](schema/evidence-record.schema.json),
[assessment/schema/assessment-result.schema.json](schema/assessment-result.schema.json),
and [assessment/schema/maturity-assessment.schema.json](schema/maturity-assessment.schema.json).
Non-authoritative examples are published at
[assessment/examples/evidence-record.example.json](examples/evidence-record.example.json),
[assessment/examples/assessment-result.example.json](examples/assessment-result.example.json),
and [assessment/examples/maturity-assessment.example.json](examples/maturity-assessment.example.json).

The contract validation command is
`python tools/validate_assessment.py --check`. It shall validate contracts and
examples only; it shall not score an organization, infer determinations, or
author findings.
