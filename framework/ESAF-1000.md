# ESAF-1000 Enterprise Secure AI Framework

**Document ID:** ESAF-1000  
**Version:** 0.1-alpha  
**Status:** Working Draft

## 1. Purpose

ESAF-1000 establishes a vendor-neutral enterprise standard for governing, securing, architecting, adopting, operating, and assuring artificial intelligence.

## 2. Scope

The standard applies to AI capabilities developed, acquired, integrated, operated, or materially used by an organization, including generative AI, machine learning, analytical models, embedded AI, retrieval-augmented generation, copilots, agents, and third-party AI services.

## 3. Core pillars

- **Protect AI** addresses security, privacy, resilience, and trustworthy operation.
- **Utilize AI** addresses responsible adoption, workforce enablement, delivery, operations, and value realization.
- **Govern AI** addresses accountability, risk, compliance, portfolio oversight, and assurance.

## 4. Enterprise AI lifecycle

1. Strategy
2. Ideation
3. Business Case
4. Risk Classification
5. Architecture
6. Data Readiness
7. Model Selection
8. Development
9. Validation
10. Approval
11. Deployment
12. Operations
13. Monitoring
14. Continuous Improvement
15. Retirement

Every normative requirement shall map to an accountable owner, applicable lifecycle stages, required evidence, and an assessment method.

## 5. Planned normative structure

The next revision will expand organizational context, leadership, planning, support, lifecycle governance, the three pillars, performance evaluation, improvement, and conformance. Detailed controls remain authoritative in ESAF-1100 rather than being duplicated here.
