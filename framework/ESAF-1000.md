# ESAF-1000 Enterprise Secure AI Framework

**Document ID:** ESAF-1000  
**Title:** Enterprise Secure AI Framework  
**Version:** 0.1-alpha  
**Status:** Working Draft  

---

## 1. Introduction

The Enterprise Secure AI Framework (ESAF) establishes a vendor-neutral standard for governing, securing, architecting, operating, and assuring Artificial Intelligence across the enterprise.

---

## 2. Core Pillars

ESAF is organized around three strategic pillars:

- Protect AI
- Utilize AI
- Govern AI
