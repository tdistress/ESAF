# ESAF-1000 Enterprise Secure AI Framework

**Document ID:** ESAF-1000  
**Title:** Enterprise Secure AI Framework  
**Version:** 0.1-alpha  
**Status:** Working Draft

## 1. Purpose

ESAF establishes a vendor-neutral enterprise standard for governing, securing, architecting, adopting, operating, and assuring artificial intelligence.

## 2. Scope

ESAF applies to AI capabilities developed, acquired, configured, operated, or materially relied upon by an organization, including machine learning, generative AI, retrieval-augmented generation, AI-enabled applications, embedded AI, and autonomous agents.

## 3. Core pillars

- **Protect AI:** Secure AI systems, data, models, applications, infrastructure, integrations, and agents.
- **Utilize AI:** Enable responsible and measurable adoption across enterprise functions.
- **Govern AI:** Establish oversight, accountability, risk management, compliance, and assurance.

## 4. Enterprise operating model

ESAF connects business strategy, governance, portfolio management, architecture, delivery, operations, assurance, and continuous improvement. Each capability is supported by accountable roles, processes, controls, evidence, and metrics.

## 5. Enterprise AI lifecycle

```text
Strategy -> Ideation -> Business Case -> Risk Classification
         -> Architecture -> Data Readiness -> Model Selection
         -> Development -> Validation -> Approval -> Deployment
         -> Operations -> Monitoring -> Improvement -> Retirement
```

The detailed working lifecycle is maintained in [`lifecycle.md`](lifecycle.md).

## 6. Conformance

An organization may claim alignment with ESAF when it defines its scope, implements applicable mandatory requirements or approved exceptions, maintains required evidence, and operates governance and assessment processes proportionate to risk.

Detailed normative content will be integrated during the v0.2-alpha milestone.
