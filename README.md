## Vision

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming foundational to every modern enterprise.

Organizations are deploying AI across productivity, software engineering, cybersecurity, operations, analytics, customer engagement, healthcare, financial services, media, and critical infrastructure. Yet most organizations lack a comprehensive, vendor-neutral standard for governing, securing, operating, and assessing AI at enterprise scale.

The **Enterprise Secure AI Framework (ESAF)** is an open, vendor-neutral enterprise standard designed to fill that gap.

ESAF provides a complete operating model for enterprise AI by integrating governance, architecture, cybersecurity, risk management, operations, compliance, and continuous improvement into a single, coherent framework.

---

# Why ESAF?

Existing standards solve pieces of the AI governance challenge.

| Standard / Framework | Primary Focus |
|----------------------|---------------|
| NIST AI RMF | AI Risk Management |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | AI Management Systems |
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework | Cybersecurity Governance |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information Security |
| PCI DSS | Payment Security |
| HITRUST CSF | Healthcare Assurance |
| OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications | AI Application Security |
| MITRE ATLAS | Adversarial AI |
| CIS Controls | Cybersecurity Best Practices |

**ESAF complements these standards by providing the enterprise operating model that connects them together.**

---

# Mission

Provide organizations with a practical, implementation-focused standard for securely adopting, governing, operating, and continuously improving Artificial Intelligence across the enterprise.

---

# What Makes ESAF Different?

Most frameworks organize around cybersecurity controls or compliance requirements.

**ESAF is organized around how enterprises actually adopt AI.**

Every requirement, control, architecture, governance process, assessment, and metric maps to the complete Enterprise AI Lifecycle.

```text
Strategy
    ↓
Governance
    ↓
Portfolio
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
```

# Project Philosophy

Artificial Intelligence will become embedded in every business capability, every software platform, and every critical service.

The challenge facing enterprises is no longer **whether** to adopt AI.

The challenge is **how** to adopt AI responsibly, securely, and at scale.

ESAF is founded on a simple belief:

> **Organizations should not have to choose between innovation and governance.**

The best AI programs enable both.

ESAF exists to provide a practical, vendor-neutral, implementation-focused standard that helps organizations build trustworthy AI capabilities while maintaining security, accountability, resilience, and operational excellence.

---

# Long-Term Vision

Our vision is for ESAF to become the enterprise reference standard for Artificial Intelligence, serving the same role that frameworks such as NIST CSF, ISO/IEC 27001, PCI DSS, CIS Controls, and TOGAF have served within their respective domains.

By integrating governance, security, architecture, operations, and assurance into a unified framework, ESAF aims to provide organizations with a practical roadmap for adopting AI confidently, responsibly, and securely.
