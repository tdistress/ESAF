# Enterprise Secure AI Framework (ESAF)

> **An open enterprise AI standards initiative for the secure governance, architecture, adoption, and operation of artificial intelligence**

![Version](https://img.shields.io/badge/version-0.1--alpha-orange)

## Vision

Artificial intelligence is becoming foundational to modern enterprises. Organizations are deploying AI across productivity, software engineering, cybersecurity, operations, analytics, customer engagement, healthcare, financial services, media, and critical infrastructure. Most organizations, however, lack a comprehensive, vendor-neutral standard for governing, securing, operating, and assessing AI at enterprise scale.

The **Enterprise Secure AI Framework (ESAF)** is an open, vendor-neutral standard designed to fill that gap. ESAF integrates governance, architecture, cybersecurity, risk management, operations, compliance, and continuous improvement into one coherent enterprise operating model.

## Mission

Provide organizations with a practical, implementation-focused standard for securely adopting, governing, operating, and continuously improving artificial intelligence.

## Why ESAF?

Existing standards address important parts of the AI governance challenge. ESAF complements them by connecting their requirements to a common enterprise AI lifecycle and operating model.

| Standard or framework | Primary focus |
|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF | AI risk management |
| ISO/IEC 42001 | AI management systems |
| NIST Cybersecurity Framework | Cybersecurity governance |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information security |
| PCI DSS | Payment security |
| HITRUST CSF | Healthcare assurance |
| OWASP guidance for LLM applications | AI application security |
| MITRE ATLAS | Adversarial AI |
| CIS Controls | Cybersecurity safeguards |
| UK Cyber Essentials | Foundational cyber hygiene |

## Core pillars

### Protect AI

Secure AI systems, models, infrastructure, identities, data, applications, and autonomous agents.

### Utilize AI

Enable responsible, secure, and measurable AI adoption throughout the enterprise.

### Govern AI

Establish executive oversight, enterprise risk management, accountability, compliance, and continuous governance.
