# ESAF-1300 Governance Manual

**Status:** Working Draft
**Version:** 0.1.0

## Purpose

ESAF-1300 operationalizes the leadership, governance-structure, and
lifecycle-gate requirements established by
[ESAF-1000](../framework/ESAF-1000.md) sections 5, 9, and 12. It supplies
charters, decision-rights assignments, a RACI model, gate operating detail,
an exception workflow, and artifact-register pointers that a governance
authority can adopt directly. It does not restate or reinterpret GOV control
requirements; the GOV family in
[ESAF-1100](../controls/ESAF-1100.md) remains authoritative for control
language, applicability, evidence, assessment procedures, and metrics.

## Scope

This manual applies to the governance bodies, decision rights, lifecycle
gates, exception process, and artifact-register practices used to operate the
enterprise AI management system described in ESAF-1000. It applies across
every capability tier defined in ESAF-1000 Appendix B and every GOV control
baseline (`E0`-`E4`); required rigor scales with tier and baseline rather than
applying uniformly. It does not apply to control-specific implementation
detail, which remains in ESAF-1100, or to architecture patterns, which remain
in ESAF-1200.

## Non-claims

Publication of this Working Draft:

- shall not be represented as certification, compliance, equivalence,
  endorsement, or independent assurance of any organization or capability;
- shall not be treated as authorizing production use, risk acceptance, or
  conformance for any specific AI capability;
- supplies normative operating elaboration subordinate to ESAF-1000 and
  ESAF-1100; it shall not create a control identifier, assessment procedure,
  or metric in addition to those defined by ESAF-1100; where this manual and
  ESAF-1000 or ESAF-1100 appear to conflict, ESAF-1000 and ESAF-1100 govern;
  and
- shall not be read to waive applicable law, regulation, or contractual
  obligation.

## Relationship to the ESAF library

ESAF-1000 establishes the management-system requirements this manual
operationalizes, including leadership commitment (§5), the enterprise AI
lifecycle and its gates (§9), and Govern AI outcomes (§12). ESAF-1100 remains
authoritative for the GOV control family:

| Control | Objective | Title |
|---|---|---|
| [GOV-100](../controls/GOV/GOV-100.md) | GOV-01 | Enterprise AI Governance Authority |
| [GOV-110](../controls/GOV/GOV-110.md) | GOV-02 | Enterprise AI Policy |
| [GOV-120](../controls/GOV/GOV-120.md) | GOV-03 | AI Portfolio Oversight |
| [GOV-130](../controls/GOV/GOV-130.md) | GOV-04 | AI Capability Accountability |
| [GOV-140](../controls/GOV/GOV-140.md) | GOV-05 | AI Exception Governance |

This manual supplies operating detail that GOV-100 through GOV-140 require an
organization to define, such as charter content, decision-rights mapping,
RACI assignment, and exception workflow steps. It shall not duplicate the
requirement, applicability, evidence, or assessment-procedure text published
in those controls, and a future revision shall update this manual rather than
fork GOV-family language.

## Governance charters

Each governance body operating under GOV-100 shall be established through an
approved charter. A charter shall document the body's accountability,
membership, decision rights, escalation path, quorum, and meeting cadence, as
required by GOV-100. At minimum, an organization shall charter:

| Charter | Implements | Typical membership |
|---|---|---|
| Executive AI steering authority | GOV-100, ESAF-1000 §5.3 | Executive leadership, business-unit sponsors |
| AI governance council | GOV-100, GOV-110 | Architecture, security, privacy, legal, data, risk, compliance, operations |
| Capability accountability charter | GOV-130 | Business owner, technical or service owner, supporting stakeholders |

A charter is a governance artifact under Appendix A of ESAF-1000 and shall be
approved, version-controlled, and reviewed at the cadence the charter itself
defines. Existing enterprise governance bodies may satisfy a charter
requirement when their AI responsibilities are made explicit, consistent with
GOV-100 applicability.

## Decision rights

ESAF-1000 §5.4 requires assigned and communicated decision rights for
strategy, use-case approval, architecture, data, model selection, risk
acceptance, production authorization, incidents, material change, and
retirement. This manual operationalizes that requirement with a decision-rights
matrix that each governance council shall maintain, recording for every
decision domain:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Decision domain | One ESAF-1000 §5.4 domain, or an organization-defined extension. |
| Deciding authority | The single role or body accountable for the decision, drawn from ESAF-1000 Appendix C. |
| Required consultees | Roles that shall be consulted before the decision is made. |
| Escalation trigger | The condition that elevates the decision to executive or board authority. |

Incompatible decision rights, such as a role approving its own production
authorization without independent review, shall be separated or subject to
documented compensating oversight, consistent with ESAF-1000 §5.4.

## RACI model

The following baseline RACI model assigns responsibility across the
ESAF-1000 §9.3 lifecycle gates. `R` is Responsible, `A` is Accountable
(single role per gate), `C` is Consulted, and `I` is Informed. Organizations
may extend rows and columns but shall preserve exactly one `A` per gate.

| Gate | Business owner | Technical owner | Governance council | Security & risk | Executive steering authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Concept / investment | A | C | R | I | C |
| Architecture and data | C | A | R | C | I |
| Security and risk | I | C | C | A | I |
| Production readiness | C | C | A | R | I |
| Periodic recertification | A | R | C | C | I |
| Material change | A | R | C | C | I |
| Retirement | A | R | C | I | I |

Internal audit or independent assurance is Informed at every gate and
Consulted whenever a gate decision relies on an open finding, consistent with
ESAF-1000 §13.3-§13.4.

## Lifecycle gates

ESAF-1000 §9.3 requires risk-based gates covering concept or investment,
architecture and data, security and risk, production readiness, periodic
recertification, material change, and retirement, and requires each gate
decision to record the capability and version, reviewers, decision authority,
evidence, disposition, accepted residual risk and expiration, and the next
review or trigger. This manual does not restate that record contract; a gate
decision record is not final unless every element required by ESAF-1000 §9.3
is present and the deciding authority matches the RACI assignment above.

A gate shall not be passed on the basis of missing or superseded evidence. When
a capability cannot satisfy a gate criterion, the deciding authority shall
either withhold progression or route the gap through the exception process
below; a gate record that reflects an active exception shall reference the
exception identifier.

## Exceptions

GOV-140 is authoritative for exception requirements: scope, justification,
risk, compensating measures, accountable owner, approval authority,
monitoring, remediation, expiration, and review conditions. This manual
operationalizes GOV-140 with the following workflow, which an organization
shall implement without weakening any GOV-140 element:

1. **Request** - the accountable capability owner submits scope,
   justification, and proposed compensating measures.
2. **Risk review** - risk and compliance evaluate residual risk and
   recommend an approval tier.
3. **Approval** - authority commensurate with residual risk approves,
   modifies, or rejects the request, consistent with GOV-140 and the decision
   rights above.
4. **Register entry** - the exception is recorded in the exception register
   required by GOV-140 with a stable identifier, owner, and expiration.
5. **Monitoring** - compensating measures are monitored for the exception
   duration; upcoming and expired exceptions are escalated automatically.
6. **Closure or renewal** - the exception is closed on remediation or
   explicitly renewed before expiration; it shall not lapse silently.

Approval-tier assignment shall scale with the capability tier defined in
ESAF-1000 Appendix B: higher-tier capabilities require higher approval
authority for an equivalent residual-risk level.

## Artifacts register

ESAF-1000 §8.2 designates the authoritative AI inventory as the system of
record for capability data, and Appendix A enumerates the governance
artifacts required when applicable to a capability. This manual does not
define a second artifact catalog. Each inventory record shall reference its
current charter, decision-rights matrix version, applicable RACI assignment,
open and closed exceptions, and gate decision records, so that a reviewer can
navigate from a capability to every governance artifact through the
inventory. An organization may publish a supplementary governance-artifact
index scoped to a business unit or capability tier, but that index shall
reference, and shall not duplicate or contradict, the ESAF-1000 Appendix A
artifact definitions or the underlying inventory record.

## Records

Governance records produced under this manual include approved charters,
decision-rights matrix versions, RACI assignment updates, gate decision
records, and exception-register entries. Each record shall be attributable,
version-controlled, retained per the documented-information requirements in
ESAF-1000 §7.5, and traceable to the accountable capability, the GOV control
it operationalizes, and the evidence relied upon, consistent with the
governance-records requirement in ESAF-1000 §12.11. A superseded record shall
be retained rather than deleted, and a change to scope, decision authority, or
accepted risk shall be recorded as a new version rather than a silent edit.
