# ESAF name and marks

The copyright licenses do not grant trademark rights or permission to imply project approval.

This policy permits truthful references to ESAF and unmodified ESAF publications. For example, a product or document may state that it implements, references, or is based on a specified ESAF publication and version when that statement is accurate.

Any modified works shall identify their changes and shall not present themselves as an official ESAF publication. Without written authorization, a person or organization shall not claim that a work, product, service, assessment, or organization is:

- sponsored by the ESAF Project Maintainers;
- endorsed by the ESAF Project Maintainers;
- certified by the ESAF Project Maintainers;
- an official ESAF publication; or
- ESAF conformant.

This policy does not create a certification program and does not claim that any ESAF mark is registered.
