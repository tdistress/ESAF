# ESAF-1300 example pack (informative)

Non-normative worksheets supporting
[ESAF-1300](../../governance/ESAF-1300.md). Completing these materials does
not establish certification, compliance, or control satisfaction.

## Worksheets

| File | ESAF-1300 section | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [charter-worksheet.md](charter-worksheet.md) | Governance charters | Blank working tables for the three charter types and minimum charter fields |
| [decision-rights-matrix.example.md](decision-rights-matrix.example.md) | Decision rights | Fictional filled assignments showing authority, consultation, escalation, and incompatible-rights separation |
| [exception-workflow.example.md](exception-workflow.example.md) | Exceptions | Fictional request-to-closure walkthrough with GOV-140 field coverage |

## Use and boundaries

These files may be copied and adapted for implementation. They are
informative, not conformance evidence, and do not replace:

- the normative requirements in ESAF-1000;
- the GOV controls in ESAF-1100;
- an organization's approved, version-controlled records; or
- assessment of control design and operating effectiveness.

Names, identifiers, dates, and decisions in filled examples are fictional.
