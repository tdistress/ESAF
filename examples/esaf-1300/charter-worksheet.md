# ESAF-1300 charter worksheet (informative)

This worksheet is non-normative enablement for
[ESAF-1300](../../governance/ESAF-1300.md) governance charters. Completing it
does not establish certification, compliance, or control satisfaction.

It provides working space for the three charter types described by ESAF-1300.
Adapt the labels to the organization's governance structure. The approved
charter and evidence retained under [GOV-100](../../controls/GOV/GOV-100.md),
not this worksheet, determine whether the control requirement is satisfied.
