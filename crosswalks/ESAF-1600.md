# ESAF-1600 Standards Crosswalk Methodology

**Status:** Working Draft

**Version:** 0.1.0

## 1. Purpose and scope

ESAF-1600 defines the authoritative method for recording versioned, reviewable, and machine-validatable analytical relationships between ESAF controls and provisions in external publications. It governs mapping-set snapshots, provision inventories, mapping records, release-pinned control manifests, lifecycle records, and generated catalogs.

Mappings shall use the finest publishable requirement identifier. Clause or domain granularity may be used only with a specific `granularity_exception`. This methodology does not create substantive mappings by itself.

## 2. Authority boundaries

ESAF-1600 Markdown records are authoritative for external mapping assertions. ESAF control records remain authoritative for control requirements, versions, and status. External authorities remain authoritative for their own publications and interpretations.

Generated `crosswalks/catalog.json` and `crosswalks/CATALOG.md` are derivative views and shall not be edited as authoritative content. A mapping shall not override an external authority, assessor, auditor, contract, or applicable law.

## 3. Record model and repository paths

An authoritative snapshot shall occupy exactly:

`crosswalks/mappings/<authority-id>/<source-version-id>/<esaf-release-id>/<mapping-set-version>/`

The directory shall contain `README.md`, `PROVISION_INVENTORY.md`, `ESAF_CONTROL_MANIFEST.json`, and direct-child provision records named `<record-id>.md`. It shall contain no subdirectory, symbolic link, or auxiliary file. Lifecycle records shall be stored as `crosswalks/registry/<mapping-set-id>.md`.

Snapshot and provision Markdown shall use YAML front matter conforming to the corresponding schema in `crosswalks/schema/`. Templates outside `crosswalks/mappings/` and `crosswalks/registry/` are non-authoritative and shall not be discovered as records.

## 4. Canonical identifiers

Canonical slugs shall contain lowercase ASCII letters and digits separated by single hyphens. Version identifiers may also contain periods. A mapping-set version shall contain exactly three nonnegative numeric components.

`mapping_set_id` shall be `<authority-id>--<publication-id>--<source-version-id>--esaf-<esaf-release-id>--<mapping-set-version>`. Double hyphens are reserved delimiters and shall not occur within a component. Snapshot path components and metadata shall agree exactly.

A provision `record_id` shall be stable within its mapping set, its filename shall be `<record-id>.md`, and its global key shall be `<mapping-set-id>/<record-id>`. The exact punctuation-sensitive external provision identifier shall remain in `external_provision_id`.

## 5. Completeness

Every mapping set shall declare `complete_publication` or `declared_subset` scope and an authoritative provision inventory. A complete-publication inventory shall enumerate every provision at the declared granularity, including provisions assessed as out of scope. A declared subset shall enumerate every provision in the disclosed subset and shall not be presented as publication-wide coverage.

Every record shall match exactly one inventory identifier. Draft snapshots may omit inventoried records but shall not add records outside the inventory. Reviewed and approved snapshots shall have exactly one record for every inventory identifier, with `expected_count`, `provision_ids`, snapshot scope, and record set in agreement. An approved snapshot shall contain at least one provision record.

## 6. Mapping taxonomy

Relationship, direction, coverage, and confidence are independent dimensions and shall not be collapsed into a strength score.

- `supports` means the source contributes directly without being independently sufficient.
- `partially_supports` means it addresses a defined subset.
- `complements` means it addresses an adjacent useful outcome.
- `prerequisite` means it establishes a condition needed by the target.
- `informs` means it supplies interpretive context without claiming coverage.

Direction shall be `esaf_to_external` or `external_to_esaf`. Bidirectional analysis shall use two independently assessed legs. Coverage shall be `substantial`, `partial`, `narrow`, or `contextual`. Confidence shall be `high`, `medium`, or `low` and describes confidence in the analysis, not in an implementation.

Every leg shall identify the ESAF control and version and shall record its own rationale, conditions, expected evidence, and known gaps. The relationship vocabulary shall not include `equivalent`, `satisfies`, or `certifies`.

## 7. Negative dispositions

Every provision shall declare exactly one disposition. `mapped` requires one or more relationship legs and prohibits `negative_rationale`. `no_direct_mapping` and `out_of_scope` require an empty `relationships` array and a specific `negative_rationale` explaining the assessed gap or scope boundary.

An omitted provision shall not be interpreted as a negative assessment. Negative dispositions are first-class results and shall remain visible in generated catalogs.

## 8. Review and findings

Snapshot editorial states shall progress `draft` to `reviewed` to `approved`; provision records shall progress `draft` to `reviewed`. Reviewed content shall identify a qualified reviewer, different from the mapper, who had authorized source access. Approved snapshots shall contain only reviewed provision records and shall identify an approver.

Findings shall record a stable identifier, affected records, severity, status, description, disposition, and required resolution or acceptance metadata. Reviewed provision records and reviewed or approved snapshots shall have no open Critical or Important findings. Approval shall also be blocked by every open Minor finding and every accepted Critical or Important finding. Only Minor findings may be accepted, with a named acceptor and rationale.

Every nonempty `affected_record_ids` entry shall resolve to a provision record in the same mapping set. An empty array targets the snapshot. Orphan finding targets are invalid in every editorial state.

Draft snapshots are mutable. Approved snapshots are immutable; corrections or changed source or ESAF releases shall create a new versioned snapshot.

## 9. Publication rights

Before source-derived content enters the repository, a named rights reviewer different from the mapper shall approve the publication basis. Each mapping set shall identify the official source and version, access class, licensing note, permitted and prohibited elements, restrictions, review date, and explicit `reviewer_authorized_source_access: true` and `publication_basis_reviewed: true` attestations. Permitted and prohibited elements shall be disjoint and shall exhaustively partition the six schema elements. Identifiers, structural inventory, and official links shall be permitted for every committed snapshot; titles, paraphrases, and derivative analysis shall additionally be permitted whenever that content is committed.

Restricted or licensed requirement text shall not be committed. Records shall use `identifier_only` when a summary is not permitted and shall state an omission rationale. Published paraphrases and derivative analysis shall be original and within the recorded rights. The record model intentionally provides no verbatim-requirement field.

## 10. Manifest provenance

Every snapshot shall pin a full 40-character ESAF source commit SHA, a control-catalog SHA-256 digest, and `ESAF_CONTROL_MANIFEST.json`. An optional tag is a display alias only and shall resolve to the pinned commit.

The manifest shall be regenerated from `VERSION.md`, `controls/catalog.json`, and eligible control records at that immutable Git object. It shall record each control's ID, version, status, path, and exact record digest. Relationships shall resolve to that manifest, not to mutable current control metadata. The committed manifest shall match deterministic regeneration byte for byte.

## 11. Snapshot and event digests

The snapshot digest shall cover every permitted direct-child file. Snapshot files shall be UTF-8 without a byte-order mark and shall contain no CR or CRLF line endings. The validator shall hash each permitted file's exact bytes, including its actual end-of-file state, sort repository-relative POSIX paths, serialize `<lowercase-sha256><two spaces><path><LF>`, and hash the resulting manifest bytes.

Lifecycle event hashing shall process, in order, `event_id`, `state`, `date`, `actor`, `reason`, `predecessor_id`, `successor_id`, `approval_reference`, and `previous_event_digest`. Missing optional values shall be empty strings. Each value shall be normalized to Unicode NFC and UTF-8 encoded; each field shall serialize as `<field>:<base-10-byte-length>:<value><LF>`. `event_digest` shall be the lowercase SHA-256 of those canonical bytes. The genesis previous digest shall be 64 zeroes; later events shall name the preceding digest.

Digest agreement alone shall not establish immutability. Validation against a trusted Git baseline shall require approved snapshot bytes to be unchanged and existing lifecycle events to remain an exact prefix.

## 12. Lifecycle

Every mapping set, whether draft, reviewed, or approved, shall have exactly one lifecycle record in the append-only registry. Its `snapshot_digest` shall match the current snapshot and shall become immutable with snapshot approval. Draft and reviewed mapping sets shall have an empty event array. An approved mapping set's event array shall be an append-only valid prefix of `approved`, `published`, `deprecated`, `retired` and shall not be empty. The current terminal state may be any state in that ordered sequence, and an event may be appended only as the next state. Event dates shall be valid calendar dates and nondecreasing.

Publication shall expose an approved snapshot. Deprecation shall name a successor or explain why none exists. Retirement shall retain the historical record while excluding it from current views. For an authority, publication, external source version, and ESAF release tuple, at most one mapping-set version may be actively published.

Every nonempty predecessor or successor identifier shall resolve to another mapping set and shall not be a self-reference. A successor link shall be reciprocated by the successor mapping set's `predecessor_id`; a mapping-set predecessor shall be reciprocated by the predecessor lifecycle's successor link. Conflicting predecessor or successor values are invalid.

A new exact source version does not invalidate an older historical version. `inconsistent` metadata is invalid, `superseded` is valid historical state, and `generated_stale` is invalid derivative output.

## 13. Validation

Repository validation shall combine JSON Schema checks with semantic, reference, provenance, rights, completeness, digest, lifecycle, baseline, link, encoding, and catalog-freshness checks. It shall fail closed on malformed, incomplete, ambiguous, stale, unauthorized, or internally inconsistent records.

Validation shall inspect every entry beneath `crosswalks/mappings/`; only the root `.gitkeep`, directory ancestors of valid snapshot roots, and permitted direct snapshot files are allowed. Duplicate YAML mapping keys at any nesting level are invalid. Authoritative metadata dates shall be real calendar dates. Broken local links and encoding-corruption signatures are invalid in every editorial state; unresolved drafting markers are invalid in reviewed and approved content.

`python tools/validate_crosswalks.py --write` shall validate authoritative records before deterministically rewriting catalogs. `python tools/validate_crosswalks.py --check` shall validate records and require generated outputs to be current. Protected-content validation shall also supply `--baseline-ref <trusted-commit>`.

## 14. Generated catalogs

The machine catalog shall retain complete snapshot, inventory, provision, relationship-leg, negative-disposition, and lifecycle data plus deterministic counts. The human catalog shall distinguish active publications, work in progress, and deprecated or retired history and shall link to authoritative records.

Catalog ordering shall be deterministic across authority, publication, source version, ESAF release, mapping-set version, external provision, record, control, and direction. An empty catalog shall report zero assessed mapping sets and shall not imply that any external publication was assessed.

## 15. Adoption disclaimers

An ESAF-1600 relationship is analytical traceability, not an implementation assessment. Adoption of a mapping set shall not establish certification, compliance, equivalence, or legal sufficiency. It shall not calculate a compliance percentage, replace qualified professional judgment, or prove that an organization has implemented either source.

Users shall verify the applicable publication version, licensing terms, jurisdiction, contract, implementation evidence, and current source-authority guidance before relying on a mapping.
