# Crosswalk Methodology

ESAF crosswalks express alignment, not certification or legal equivalence.

Each mapping shall identify:

- ESAF control or objective
- External requirement and version
- Relationship: direct, partial, supporting, or contextual
- Rationale
- Reviewer and review date
- Known gaps or conditions

Mappings shall use authoritative source material and shall be revalidated when either ESAF or the external standard changes.
