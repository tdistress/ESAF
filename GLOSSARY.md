# ESAF Glossary

**Status:** Working Draft  
**Version:** 0.1-alpha  

---

## AI Capability

A business function that uses Artificial Intelligence to produce an organizational outcome.

## AI System

The complete collection of software, models, data, infrastructure, identities, workflows, integrations, and governance required to deliver an AI capability.

## AI Asset

Any component that supports an AI System, including models, datasets, prompts, vector stores, agents, APIs, plugins, tools, and knowledge bases.

## AI Bill of Materials (AIBOM)

A structured inventory of the components, dependencies, data sources, models, tools, APIs, prompts, agents, and infrastructure that comprise an AI system.

## AI Agent

An AI component capable of planning, reasoning, invoking tools, or initiating actions within defined permissions.

## Model Registry

The authoritative record of approved AI models, their versions, owners, validation status, risk classifications, and deployment history.

## Prompt

An input, instruction, system message, or template used to guide AI system behavior.

## Vector Store

A database or storage system that maintains embeddings used for semantic search or retrieval-augmented generation.

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

An AI architecture that retrieves relevant enterprise information and supplies it to a model to improve response accuracy and contextual relevance.

## Enterprise AI Lifecycle

The standard lifecycle through which AI capabilities progress from strategy through retirement.
