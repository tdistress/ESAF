# MON-140 AI Alert Response

## Requirement

The organization shall assign ownership, priority, triage, investigation, escalation, containment, tuning, documentation, and service-level requirements to AI security, behavior, operational, and compliance alerts.

## Intent

Telemetry provides no assurance unless alerts produce timely and accountable response.

## Applicability

Applies to automated and manually generated alerts across AI and supporting services.

## Implementation guidance

Define severity, routing, coverage, correlated context, false-positive tuning, emergency action, handoff, closure criteria, and feedback into detection and risk.

## Expected evidence

- Alert procedures, ownership, severity, service levels, tickets, investigations, escalations, tuning, and closure.

## Assessment procedures

- **MON-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify response requirements for each alert class.
- **MON-140-A2 - Test:** Inject representative alerts and measure routing and response.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Critical alerts handled within service level | 100% | Monthly |
