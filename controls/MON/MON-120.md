# MON-120 AI Behavior and Drift Monitoring

## Requirement

The organization shall monitor production AI behavior for performance, quality, data and concept drift, safety, fairness, reliability, policy, uncertainty, and emerging limitations against approved baselines and thresholds.

## Intent

AI behavior can degrade after validation even when software and infrastructure remain unchanged.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4 according to purpose, risk, feedback availability, and affected parties.

## Implementation guidance

Define baselines, indicators, sampling, ground truth, segmented analysis, thresholds, investigation, and retraining, restriction, or rollback triggers.

## Expected evidence

- Monitoring plan, baselines, thresholds, dashboards, analyses, alerts, findings, and actions.

## Assessment procedures

- **MON-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify monitored dimensions and thresholds against validation.
- **MON-120-A2 - Test:** Recalculate selected measures and threshold decisions.
