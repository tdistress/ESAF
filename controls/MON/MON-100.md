# MON-100 AI Telemetry

## Requirement

The organization shall collect attributable, time-synchronized, protected, and risk-proportionate telemetry sufficient to monitor AI identity, input, context, retrieval, model, output, tool, action, configuration, performance, cost, and policy events.

## Intent

AI assurance requires visibility across the request and action chain rather than infrastructure metrics alone.

## Applicability

Applies according to risk, privacy, security, retention, and technical feasibility.

## Implementation guidance

Define event schemas, correlation identifiers, actor, time, model version, policy decision, outcome, error, latency, and resource use. Minimize sensitive content and document visibility gaps.

## Expected evidence

- Telemetry standard, event inventory, schemas, sample traces, access, integrity, retention, and gap decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **MON-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify coverage against required event categories.
- **MON-100-A2 - Test:** Trace representative requests and actions across telemetry sources.
