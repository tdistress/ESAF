# MON-150 AI Audit Trails

## Requirement

The organization shall preserve protected and attributable audit trails for material AI access, approvals, configuration, model and data changes, policy decisions, privileged activity, agent actions, incidents, and governance decisions for the required retention period.

## Intent

Durable evidence enables accountability, investigation, assessment, and dispute resolution.

## Applicability

Applies according to risk, legal, privacy, security, and records requirements.

## Implementation guidance

Define event scope, identity, time, correlation, integrity, access, retention, legal hold, export, deletion, and archive.

## Expected evidence

- Audit standard, configurations, sample trails, integrity controls, access reviews, retention, and retrieval tests.

## Assessment procedures

- **MON-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify audit coverage and retention against requirements.
- **MON-150-A2 - Test:** Retrieve and reconstruct sampled material events.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Required audit events retained and retrievable | 100% | Quarterly |
