# MOD-100 Model Governance and Registry

## Requirement

The organization shall register every material model with a unique identifier, owner, approved purpose, provider, version, lifecycle state, risk classification, validation status, deployment relationships, review date, and retirement status before approved use.

## Intent

An authoritative registry supports ownership, traceability, change control, incident analysis, and retirement.

## Applicability

Applies to foundation, fine-tuned, embedded, open, commercial, internally developed, and task-specific models.

## Implementation guidance

Link model records to capabilities, datasets, evaluations, AIBOMs, deployments, suppliers, approvals, and incidents. Distinguish provider marketing names from immutable model and release identifiers.

## Expected evidence

- Model registry records, ownership, approvals, deployment links, reviews, and reconciliation results.

## Assessment procedures

- **MOD-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify completeness and approval of sampled model records.
- **MOD-100-A2 - Test:** Reconcile deployed model identifiers with the registry.
- **MOD-100-A3 - Interview:** Confirm registry ownership and update triggers.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Material models registered before use | 100% | Continuous |
| Deployed models with stale or unmatched records | 0 | Monthly |
