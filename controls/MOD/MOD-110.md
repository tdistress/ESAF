# MOD-110 Model Provenance and Supply Chain

## Requirement

The organization shall establish and verify provenance, source, licensing, usage rights, integrity, dependencies, known limitations, supplier terms, and material development or modification history for each model before approved use.

## Intent

Unverified model origins and terms create security, legal, quality, and supply-chain exposure.

## Applicability

Applies to acquired, downloaded, hosted, embedded, internally trained, fine-tuned, quantized, converted, or otherwise modified models.

## Implementation guidance

Use trusted repositories, signed or hashed artifacts, supplier attestations, model cards, dependency manifests, licenses, and acquisition records. Document gaps and restrict unverifiable models according to risk.

## Expected evidence

- Source and acquisition records, licenses, terms, integrity values, dependency records, supplier evidence, and provenance review.

## Assessment procedures

- **MOD-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify provenance and rights for sampled models.
- **MOD-110-A2 - Test:** Validate artifact integrity against an approved value or source.
- **MOD-110-A3 - Examine:** Trace material modifications to authorized records and updated evidence.
