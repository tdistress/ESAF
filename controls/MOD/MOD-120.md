# MOD-120 Model Validation

## Requirement

The organization shall validate each model against approved, risk-proportionate criteria for intended performance, limitations, safety, security, robustness, fairness, privacy, explainability, resilience, and operating conditions before authorization and after material change.

## Intent

Model selection and deployment decisions require evidence beyond general benchmarks or supplier claims.

## Applicability

Applies to initial selection, fine-tuning, configuration, major provider updates, material context changes, and periodic revalidation.

## Implementation guidance

Use representative and adversarial tests, defined acceptance thresholds, independent challenge for E3/E4, uncertainty analysis, and documented test limitations. Separate validation data from training where appropriate.

## Expected evidence

- Validation plan, test data authority, criteria, results, limitations, findings, approvals, and residual-risk decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **MOD-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify that validation covers applicable criteria and approved conditions.
- **MOD-120-A2 - Test:** Reperform selected tests and compare results.
- **MOD-120-A3 - Examine:** Trace failed criteria to remediation, restriction, or acceptance.
