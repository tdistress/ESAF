# MOD-140 Model Protection and Integrity

## Requirement

The organization shall protect model artifacts, weights, parameters, configurations, and restricted interfaces from unauthorized access, extraction, modification, replacement, deletion, disclosure, and unapproved duplication.

## Intent

Models may embody intellectual property, sensitive training influence, security controls, and privileged capability.

## Applicability

Applies to stored, transferred, cached, deployed, backed-up, and retired model artifacts and management interfaces.

## Implementation guidance

Apply classification, encryption, least privilege, signing or hashing, protected registries, deployment verification, egress control, rate limits, extraction monitoring, backups, and tamper alerts according to risk.

## Expected evidence

- Protection design, access policies, cryptographic configuration, integrity checks, alerts, extraction tests, and incident records.

## Assessment procedures

- **MOD-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify controls for model storage, transfer, deployment, and administration.
- **MOD-140-A2 - Test:** Attempt unauthorized retrieval or replacement of a representative model artifact.
- **MOD-140-A3 - Test:** Validate integrity detection before deployment and during operation.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized model artifact access or change | 0 | Continuous |
| Production deployments with verified integrity | 100% | Each deployment |
