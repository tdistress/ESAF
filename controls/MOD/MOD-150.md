# MOD-150 Model Retirement

## Requirement

The organization shall retire a model through an authorized process that identifies affected capabilities, removes deployment and access, preserves required evidence, disposes of artifacts and data, updates dependencies and records, and verifies that prohibited use cannot continue.

## Intent

Retired models can remain reachable through caches, endpoints, backups, integrations, or unmanaged copies.

## Applicability

Applies to end-of-life, replaced, unsupported, compromised, nonconforming, expired, and abandoned models.

## Implementation guidance

Coordinate capability owners, suppliers, registries, platforms, endpoints, archives, legal retention, and continuity. Define transition and rollback windows without leaving indefinite unauthorized access.

## Expected evidence

- Retirement approval, dependency impact, migration, access removal, endpoint removal, disposition, registry update, and verification records.

## Assessment procedures

- **MOD-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify complete retirement evidence for sampled models.
- **MOD-150-A2 - Test:** Attempt access through former endpoints, credentials, and deployment paths.
- **MOD-150-A3 - Test:** Reconcile retired registry state with active infrastructure and dependencies.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Retired models remaining accessible outside approved retention | 0 | Monthly |
| Retirement actions completed by approved date | 100% | Monthly |
