# AGT-150 Agent Intervention and Recovery

## Requirement

The organization shall provide, protect, monitor, and test mechanisms to pause, restrict, isolate, terminate, roll back, recover, and safely resume agent operation according to defined triggers and authority.

## Intent

Agents capable of action require intervention that remains effective during model error, compromise, runaway execution, or provider failure.

## Applicability

Applies to individual agents, workflows, tool access, credentials, queues, scheduled tasks, and dependent integrations.

## Implementation guidance

Place controls outside the agent, define fail-safe state, revoke credentials and queued work, preserve evidence, prevent automatic restart, validate downstream rollback, and assign emergency authority.

## Expected evidence

- Intervention design, triggers, authorities, runbooks, isolation, credential revocation, rollback, recovery tests, and incidents.

## Assessment procedures

- **AGT-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify intervention coverage and fail-safe behavior.
- **AGT-150-A2 - Test:** Exercise pause, isolation, termination, and credential revocation.
- **AGT-150-A3 - Test:** Recover or roll back a representative action and verify safe resumption.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Applicable agents with successful intervention test | 100% | Risk-defined |
| Agents restarting after emergency stop without authorization | 0 | Continuous |
