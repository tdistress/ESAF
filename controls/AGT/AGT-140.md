# AGT-140 Agent Action Traceability

## Requirement

The organization shall record sufficient attributable evidence of agent instructions, relevant context, plans where available, tool calls, permissions, approvals, actions, outputs, errors, and outcomes to support monitoring, investigation, reconstruction, and accountability.

## Intent

Agent actions span systems and require end-to-end evidence rather than isolated model logs.

## Applicability

Applies according to action risk, privacy, security, legal, and retention requirements.

## Implementation guidance

Use correlation identifiers across model, orchestrator, tool, and target systems. Protect logs from alteration, minimize unnecessary sensitive content, synchronize time, and document unavoidable observability gaps.

## Expected evidence

- Logging design, correlation, sample traces, time synchronization, access, integrity, retention, and gap decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **AGT-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify trace coverage and evidence protections.
- **AGT-140-A2 - Test:** Reconstruct a sampled agent action end to end.
- **AGT-140-A3 - Test:** Verify alerts and investigation when trace continuity is lost.
