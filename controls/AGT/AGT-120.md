# AGT-120 Agent Human Oversight

## Requirement

The organization shall define and implement risk-proportionate human approval, supervision, intervention, challenge, and appeal for agent decisions and actions, including conditions that prohibit autonomous execution.

## Intent

Human oversight must be meaningful, timely, informed, and capable of changing outcomes.

## Applicability

Applies according to action impact, reversibility, uncertainty, affected parties, legal duties, and safety consequences.

## Implementation guidance

Define human-in, on, or over-the-loop patterns by action class. Provide sufficient context, limitations, time, authority, and interface design. Prevent approval fatigue and prohibit self-approval by the acting agent.

## Expected evidence

- Oversight model, action classes, approval workflows, reviewer training, intervention tests, appeals, and override records.

## Assessment procedures

- **AGT-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify oversight assignments and prohibited-autonomy conditions.
- **AGT-120-A2 - Test:** Exercise approval, rejection, intervention, and appeal paths.
- **AGT-120-A3 - Observe:** Observe a reviewer handling a representative high-impact action.
