# STR-120 Controlled AI Experimentation

## Requirement

The organization shall authorize AI experiments through a documented, time-bounded process that assigns an owner, defines the hypothesis and boundary, restricts data and access, prevents unapproved material outcomes, specifies monitoring, and requires closure or formal lifecycle promotion.

## Intent

Streamlined experimentation enables learning without allowing prototypes to become ungoverned production services.

## Applicability

Applies to E0 research, proofs of concept, evaluations, red-team environments, and pilots that do not yet have production authorization.

## Implementation guidance

Use segregated environments and synthetic, public, or specifically approved data. Define expiry, cost and resource limits, prohibited integrations, participant access, evidence retention, and promotion criteria. Reclassify the capability if scope or impact expands.

## Expected evidence

- Experiment request, owner, hypothesis, risk screen, approved boundary, data authorization, expiry, monitoring, results, and closure or promotion record.

## Assessment procedures

- **STR-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify that sampled experiments contain all required authorizations and boundaries.
- **STR-120-A2 - Test:** Identify expired or externally exposed experiments and verify closure or escalation.
- **STR-120-A3 - Observe:** Confirm that technical segregation and data restrictions match approvals.
