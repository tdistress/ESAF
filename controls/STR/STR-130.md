# STR-130 AI Resource Planning

## Requirement

The organization shall identify, approve, and periodically review the people, competencies, funding, data, platforms, assurance, operational capacity, and supplier resources required to achieve AI objectives and maintain controls throughout the lifecycle.

## Intent

Under-resourced governance and operations create hidden risk, unreliable controls, and unsustainable adoption.

## Applicability

Applies to enterprise planning and material AI initiatives. Resource depth scales with portfolio size, capability risk, and reliance on shared services.

## Implementation guidance

Integrate resource planning with budget, workforce, architecture, procurement, continuity, and portfolio processes. Address scarce skills, supplier dependence, monitoring costs, testing environments, data stewardship, and retirement funding.

## Expected evidence

- Resource plans, budgets, staffing and competency analysis, capacity forecasts, supplier plans, and remediation actions.

## Assessment procedures

- **STR-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify that plans address every required resource category and align to objectives.
- **STR-130-A2 - Interview:** Confirm how identified shortages are prioritized and escalated.
- **STR-130-A3 - Test:** Trace selected critical resource gaps to funded treatment or accepted risk.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Critical resource gaps without approved treatment | 0 | Quarterly |
| Required AI roles meeting competency criteria | Organization-defined | Quarterly |
