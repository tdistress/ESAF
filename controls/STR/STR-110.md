# STR-110 AI Value Realization

## Requirement

The accountable business owner shall define, measure, and periodically evaluate each production AI capability's intended outcomes, costs, material adverse effects, and continuation criteria using approved measures established before production authorization.

## Intent

AI capabilities should remain in operation only when evidence supports their intended value relative to cost, risk, and unintended effects.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4 production capabilities. E0 experiments use hypothesis and learning measures under STR-120.

## Implementation guidance

Use business and stakeholder outcomes rather than activity counts alone. Establish a baseline and measurement period where feasible. Define thresholds for improvement, restriction, suspension, or retirement.

## Expected evidence

- Business case, approved measures, baseline, outcome reports, cost data, adverse-effect indicators, and continuation decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **STR-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify that measures and continuation criteria were approved before production.
- **STR-110-A2 - Test:** Recalculate a sample of reported measures from source data.
- **STR-110-A3 - Test:** Trace underperforming capabilities to documented management decisions.

## Metrics
