# STR-100 Enterprise AI Strategy Alignment

## Requirement

The organization shall maintain approved AI objectives and investment priorities that trace to organizational strategy, stakeholder requirements, risk appetite, measurable outcomes, and an accountable executive sponsor.

## Intent

Strategic traceability prevents disconnected technology adoption and directs resources toward justified outcomes within risk tolerance.

## Applicability

Applies to enterprise AI strategy and material portfolio investments. Experiments may trace to an approved innovation objective rather than a production business objective.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain an AI strategy or integrate AI objectives into existing strategy. Define planning horizon, priorities, dependencies, constraints, risk appetite, and measures. Review when business strategy or material external conditions change.

## Expected evidence

- Approved strategy, objectives, roadmap, investment criteria, and sponsorship records.
- Traceability from sampled initiatives to an approved objective and risk appetite.

## Assessment procedures

- **STR-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify that objectives include required traceability and ownership.
- **STR-100-A2 - Test:** Trace selected portfolio initiatives to approved priorities and outcomes.
- **STR-100-A3 - Interview:** Confirm how leadership resolves investments outside strategy or risk appetite.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Material initiatives traceable to an approved objective | 100% | Quarterly |
| Strategy review completed when due | 100% | Annual and event-driven |
