# AUD-130 AI Findings and Remediation

## Requirement

The organization shall document, classify, assign, prioritize, remediate, escalate, retest, close, and retain evidence for AI assessment findings according to risk, root cause, affected scope, due date, and acceptance authority.

## Intent

Findings improve controls only when accountable actions correct causes and effectiveness is verified.

## Applicability

Applies to audits, assessments, validation, red teaming, testing, incidents, complaints, and supplier assurance.

## Implementation guidance

Identify systemic impact, related capabilities, compensating controls, overdue escalation, recurrence, and residual risk. Require independent closure for material findings where practical.

## Expected evidence

- Finding records, severity, root cause, owner, plan, due date, mitigation, acceptance, retest, closure, and recurrence analysis.

## Assessment procedures

- **AUD-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify completeness and risk treatment for sampled findings.
- **AUD-130-A2 - Test:** Reperform closure tests and confirm affected scope was addressed.
