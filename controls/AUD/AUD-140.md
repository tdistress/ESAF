# AUD-140 AI Management Review and Assurance Reporting

## Requirement

Executive leadership shall review the AI management system at planned intervals using current information on context, objectives, portfolio, risk, incidents, exceptions, obligations, control effectiveness, assessments, suppliers, resources, and improvement needs and record resulting decisions.

## Intent

Management review converts assurance into accountable decisions on direction, risk, resources, and improvement.

## Applicability

Applies to the enterprise AI management system and aggregated portfolio.

## Implementation guidance

Define review frequency and escalation by risk. Distinguish facts, estimates, uncertainty, and judgment. Record decisions on policy, scope, objectives, resources, treatments, corrective actions, and acceptance.

## Expected evidence

- Review inputs, agenda, participants, reports, decisions, actions, owners, due dates, and follow-up.

## Assessment procedures

- **AUD-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify completeness, cadence, authority, and retained decisions.
- **AUD-140-A2 - Test:** Trace sampled management actions through completion or escalation.
