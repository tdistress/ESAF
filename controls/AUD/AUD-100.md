# AUD-100 AI Assessment Program

## Requirement

The organization shall maintain a risk-based program that defines scope, criteria, frequency, methods, sampling, responsibilities, independence, evidence, reporting, and follow-up for assessing the AI management system, capabilities, and controls.

## Intent

Planned assurance provides repeatable evidence of design and operating effectiveness.

## Applicability

Applies to enterprise governance and in-scope capabilities, with frequency and depth based on risk, change, incidents, and prior findings.

## Implementation guidance

Coordinate self-assessment, control testing, technical validation, internal audit, supplier assurance, and independent review. Avoid duplicate testing while preserving scope and independence.

## Expected evidence

- Assessment universe, plan, risk rationale, scopes, procedures, samples, reports, follow-up, and coverage analysis.

## Assessment procedures

- **AUD-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify program coverage, risk basis, and required methods.
- **AUD-100-A2 - Test:** Trace selected planned assessments through completion and follow-up.
