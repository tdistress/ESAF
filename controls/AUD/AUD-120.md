# AUD-120 AI Assessment Evidence

## Requirement

The organization shall obtain and retain relevant, reliable, complete, timely, attributable, and integrity-protected evidence sufficient to support each AI assessment procedure, determination, scope, and period.

## Intent

Assertions, screenshots, or isolated artifacts alone may not demonstrate sustained control effectiveness.

## Applicability

Applies to automated and manual evidence, inherited controls, supplier assurance, sampling, and technical test results.

## Implementation guidance

Record source, owner, collection time, scope, population, sample, integrity, limitations, retention, and relationship to the result. Corroborate weak evidence and protect sensitive assessment content.

## Expected evidence

- Evidence index, collection records, samples, source validation, integrity, limitations, workpapers, and retention.

## Assessment procedures

- **AUD-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify evidence quality and traceability for sampled determinations.
- **AUD-120-A2 - Test:** Reproduce selected evidence collection and compare results.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Assessment determinations supported by sufficient evidence | 100% | Each assessment |
