# AUD-110 AI Assessor Competence and Independence

## Requirement

The organization shall assign AI assessments to personnel with documented competence and independence proportionate to the subject, method, complexity, and risk being evaluated.

## Intent

Credible assurance requires relevant expertise and freedom from self-review or improper influence.

## Applicability

Applies to governance, technical, model, data, privacy, safety, compliance, and control assessments.

## Implementation guidance

Define competency criteria by assessment type, evaluate conflicts, use multidisciplinary teams, rotate reviewers where appropriate, and obtain external expertise when internal independence or skill is insufficient.

## Expected evidence

- Role criteria, qualifications, training, experience, conflict declarations, assignments, supervision, and external-expert records.

## Assessment procedures

- **AUD-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify competence and independence for sampled assessment teams.
- **AUD-110-A2 - Interview:** Confirm conflict management and escalation in practice.
