# IAM-120 AI Authorization and Least Privilege

## Requirement

The organization shall authorize access to AI assets and actions according to approved purpose, role, attributes, context, and least privilege, including explicit limits on models, data, prompts, tools, integrations, administration, and agent actions.

## Intent

Fine-grained authorization limits misuse, lateral movement, excessive agency, data exposure, and unintended actions after authentication.

## Applicability

Applies to human and non-human access, including retrieval, inference, fine-tuning, deployment, monitoring, tool use, and administration.

## Implementation guidance

Default to deny. Separate use, development, approval, deployment, administration, and audit roles. Enforce source-document authorization during retrieval and action-level authorization at the tool or target system rather than relying on model instructions alone.

## Expected evidence

- Access model, role and attribute definitions, authorization policies, approvals, configurations, denials, and exception records.

## Assessment procedures

- **IAM-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify that access policies cover required AI assets and actions.
- **IAM-120-A2 - Test:** Attempt prohibited access and action paths using representative identities.
- **IAM-120-A3 - Examine:** Compare granted permissions with approved role and purpose.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Sampled permissions conforming to approved least privilege | 100% | Quarterly |
| Unauthorized AI actions successfully executed | 0 | Continuous |
