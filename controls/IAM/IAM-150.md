# IAM-150 AI Access Review and Revocation

## Requirement

The organization shall review access to AI assets at a risk-defined frequency and revoke or adjust access promptly when purpose, role, employment, capability scope, authorization, supplier relationship, or operational need changes.

## Intent

Access accumulates and becomes inappropriate as people, services, agents, suppliers, and AI capabilities change.

## Applicability

Applies to all identities and access grants, with higher frequency and stronger reviewer independence for privileged, E3, and E4 access.

## Implementation guidance

Define reviewer, population, frequency, evidence, escalation, and completion criteria. Include direct, group, inherited, federated, emergency, dormant, and non-human access. Trigger out-of-cycle review after incidents and material changes.

## Expected evidence

- Access-review schedule, populations, reviewer attestations, revocations, exceptions, overdue escalation, and termination records.

## Assessment procedures

- **IAM-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify that reviews cover all required identity and access types at the defined frequency.
- **IAM-150-A2 - Test:** Sample revoked or changed access and confirm timely enforcement across dependent systems.
- **IAM-150-A3 - Test:** Identify dormant, orphaned, or unauthorized access and verify disposition.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Access reviews completed by due date | 100% | Risk-defined |
| Revocations exceeding defined service level | 0 | Monthly |
