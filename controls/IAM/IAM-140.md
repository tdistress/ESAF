# IAM-140 AI Secrets Management

## Requirement

The organization shall generate, store, distribute, use, rotate, revoke, and monitor credentials, tokens, keys, and other secrets used by AI capabilities through approved secrets-management mechanisms and prevent their inclusion in prompts, source code, models, logs, or documentation.

## Intent

AI workflows frequently connect to external services and tools; exposed secrets can enable unauthorized data access and action.

## Applicability

Applies to development, testing, production, automation, agents, pipelines, APIs, plugins, and emergency access.

## Implementation guidance

Use managed secret stores and short-lived, scoped credentials. Scan repositories, prompts, logs, artifacts, and deployment packages for secrets. Define rapid revocation and rotation after exposure, personnel change, supplier incident, or capability retirement.

## Expected evidence

- Secrets standard, managed-store records, rotation policy, scanning results, exposure incidents, and revocation evidence.

## Assessment procedures

- **IAM-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify that sampled AI integrations retrieve secrets from approved services.
- **IAM-140-A2 - Test:** Scan representative repositories, prompts, logs, and artifacts for embedded secrets.
- **IAM-140-A3 - Test:** Validate rotation and revocation for a representative non-human credential.

## Metrics
