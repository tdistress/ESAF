# IAM-130 Privileged AI Access

## Requirement

The organization shall restrict, separately authenticate, time-bound where feasible, monitor, and periodically review privileged access capable of changing AI models, data, system instructions, safety controls, tools, production configuration, logs, or authorization policy.

## Intent

Privileged changes can alter AI behavior or conceal misuse and therefore require stronger safeguards and accountability.

## Applicability

Applies to administrative, emergency, deployment, model-management, platform, data, and security-control access across all baselines.

## Implementation guidance

Use separate administrative identities, privileged-access workflows, just-in-time elevation, strong authentication, session logging, approval for sensitive changes, and emergency-access review. Prevent routine productivity use from privileged sessions.

## Expected evidence

- Privileged-role definitions, approvals, elevation records, authentication configuration, session logs, alerts, and access reviews.

## Assessment procedures

- **IAM-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify that privileged functions and identities are identified and separately controlled.
- **IAM-130-A2 - Test:** Sample elevation and emergency-access events for approval, duration, logging, and review.
- **IAM-130-A3 - Observe:** Observe a privileged-access workflow for a representative AI administration task.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Privileged access events with attributable authorization | 100% | Monthly |
| Standing privileged access beyond approved need | Organization-defined threshold | Monthly |
