# IAM-110 AI Authentication

## Requirement

The organization shall authenticate identities before granting access to non-public AI assets using mechanisms whose strength, context, and resistance to replay or credential theft are proportionate to risk.

## Intent

Authentication prevents unauthenticated or impersonated access to AI systems, data, models, tools, and administrative functions.

## Applicability

Applies to users, administrators, services, workloads, APIs, agents, and external integrations. Public endpoints remain subject to abuse controls and authenticated administration.

## Implementation guidance

Use federated enterprise authentication, phishing-resistant multifactor authentication for privileged or high-impact human access, and workload-bound credentials or mutual authentication for non-human identities. Define session, reauthentication, and failure-handling requirements.

## Expected evidence

- Authentication standards, identity-provider configuration, endpoint configuration, session policies, and authentication logs.

## Assessment procedures

- **IAM-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify that authentication strength and session controls match risk criteria.
- **IAM-110-A2 - Test:** Attempt access without valid authentication and test selected authentication flows.
- **IAM-110-A3 - Examine:** Review authentication failures, bypasses, and exceptions for disposition.
