# IAM-100 AI Identity Governance

## Requirement

The organization shall uniquely identify, inventory, assign ownership to, and manage the lifecycle of every human, service, workload, API, and agent identity that can access in-scope AI assets.

## Intent

Attributable identities are the foundation for authorization, accountability, review, monitoring, and incident response.

## Applicability

Applies to interactive, non-interactive, embedded, federated, temporary, and third-party identities across all baselines.

## Implementation guidance

Use enterprise identity services where feasible. Distinguish human from non-human identities, prohibit shared identities unless formally controlled, record owner and purpose, and integrate creation, change, suspension, credential rotation, and deletion with capability lifecycle events.

## Expected evidence

- Identity inventory, ownership, purpose, type, linked capability, lifecycle status, and identity-provider records.
- Joiner, mover, leaver, service-onboarding, and decommissioning records.

## Assessment procedures

- **IAM-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify that sampled identities are unique, inventoried, owned, and linked to a capability.
- **IAM-100-A2 - Test:** Reconcile AI access records with the identity inventory and investigate unmatched identities.
- **IAM-100-A3 - Interview:** Confirm lifecycle responsibilities for human and non-human identities.

## Metrics
