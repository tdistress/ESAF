# ARC-150 AI Technology Lifecycle Architecture

## Requirement

The organization shall govern approval, adoption, currency, compatibility, technical debt, support, replacement, deprecation, and retirement of AI technologies and architecture components throughout their lifecycle.

## Intent

Rapid AI change creates unsupported components, hidden debt, incompatible versions, and unsafe continued use.

## Applicability

Applies to models, frameworks, runtimes, protocols, platforms, vector stores, orchestration, tools, interfaces, and infrastructure.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain approved and prohibited technology records, owners, versions, support dates, dependencies, debt, migration criteria, watch lists, and retirement plans. Monitor supplier roadmaps and breaking changes.

## Expected evidence

- Technology catalog, standards, lifecycle status, support dates, debt, exceptions, roadmaps, migrations, and retirement.

## Assessment procedures

- **ARC-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify lifecycle status and ownership for sampled components.
- **ARC-150-A2 - Test:** Identify unsupported or prohibited technology in deployed AI capabilities.
