# ARC-140 AI Shared Responsibility Architecture

## Requirement

The organization shall document, approve, validate, and monitor responsibility boundaries and inherited controls among capability owners, shared platforms, model providers, cloud services, data owners, tool providers, and other suppliers.

## Intent

Ambiguous shared responsibility creates control gaps and duplicate assumptions.

## Applicability

Applies whenever a control or service outcome depends on another organizational unit or external provider.

## Implementation guidance

Identify provider and consumer duties, configuration, evidence, limitations, service levels, incidents, changes, assurance, dependency, contingency, and exit. Revalidate inheritance after material change.

## Expected evidence

- Responsibility matrices, inherited-control records, evidence, configurations, reviews, gaps, and contingencies.

## Assessment procedures

- **ARC-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify responsibility and inheritance for sampled controls.
- **ARC-140-A2 - Test:** Confirm consumer configuration and provider evidence satisfy the requirement.
