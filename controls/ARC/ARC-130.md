# ARC-130 AI Resilience and Failure Design

## Requirement

The organization shall design AI capabilities for foreseeable component, model, data, provider, network, capacity, quality, and control failures using isolation, graceful degradation, safe state, fallback, reversibility, recovery, and tested resumption.

## Intent

AI failure includes unsafe or unreliable behavior as well as service unavailability.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4 according to consequence, criticality, and substitutability.

## Implementation guidance

Define failure modes, dependency timeouts, circuit breakers, non-AI fallback, human takeover, data consistency, rollback, recovery sequence, and criteria that prohibit resumption.

## Expected evidence

- Failure analysis, resilience design, safe states, fallback, recovery objectives, tests, findings, and remediation.

## Assessment procedures

- **ARC-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify design coverage for foreseeable failures.
- **ARC-130-A2 - Test:** Exercise representative failures, fallback, and safe resumption.
