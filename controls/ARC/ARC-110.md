# ARC-110 AI Trust Boundaries and Data Flows

## Requirement

The organization shall document and maintain AI components, actors, identities, data and control flows, trust levels, entry and exit points, external dependencies, administrative paths, and enforcement boundaries.

## Intent

Controls cannot be placed or assessed without a current representation of what crosses each boundary.

## Applicability

Applies to logical and physical architecture, including models, RAG, agents, tools, providers, telemetry, and human oversight.

## Implementation guidance

Show classification, purpose, protocol, authentication, authorization, encryption, retention, supplier boundary, and failure behavior. Update diagrams after material change.

## Expected evidence

- Component and context diagrams, data-flow diagrams, trust labels, interface inventory, administrative paths, and change history.

## Assessment procedures

- **ARC-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify required components, actors, flows, and boundaries.
- **ARC-110-A2 - Test:** Compare diagrams with deployed configuration and observed traffic.
