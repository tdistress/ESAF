# ARC-120 AI Reference Architecture Patterns

## Requirement

The organization shall establish, version, validate, publish, and maintain reusable reference patterns and control points for recurring AI architectures and require deviations to receive documented review.

## Intent

Validated patterns improve consistency, speed, interoperability, and control inheritance.

## Applicability

Applies to recurring patterns such as gateways, RAG, copilots, agents, private models, integrations, and observability.

## Implementation guidance

Define scope, assumptions, trust boundaries, required and optional controls, deployment variants, evidence, limitations, dependencies, and retirement status for each pattern.

## Expected evidence

- Pattern catalog, versions, validation, control mapping, usage, exceptions, reviews, and deprecation records.

## Assessment procedures

- **ARC-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify pattern content, ownership, validation, and currency.
- **ARC-120-A2 - Test:** Compare sampled implementations with the selected pattern and approved deviations.
