# ARC-100 AI Architecture Governance

## Requirement

The organization shall review, approve, record, and periodically reassess AI architecture decisions, standards, patterns, technologies, exceptions, and technical debt according to risk and lifecycle state.

## Intent

Architecture governance creates consistent control boundaries without preventing justified innovation.

## Applicability

Applies to new capabilities, material changes, shared platforms, exceptions, and retirement.

## Implementation guidance

Define review triggers, required disciplines, decision records, approved technologies, conditions, exceptions, debt ownership, expiry, and escalation. Use accelerated paths for bounded experiments.

## Expected evidence

- Architecture standards, review records, decisions, conditions, exceptions, debt, reassessments, and retirement decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **ARC-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify required architecture review and decisions for sampled capabilities.
- **ARC-100-A2 - Test:** Trace conditions and exceptions to implementation and expiry.
