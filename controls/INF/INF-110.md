# INF-110 AI Infrastructure Hardening

## Requirement

The organization shall harden AI compute, accelerators, hosts, containers, orchestration, networks, storage, images, runtimes, and management interfaces using approved, versioned, and risk-proportionate configuration baselines.

## Intent

AI platforms expand conventional attack surface through specialized compute, shared runtimes, model servers, and data services.

## Applicability

Applies to organization-managed infrastructure and configuration responsibilities in shared services.

## Implementation guidance

Remove unnecessary services, restrict management paths, isolate workloads, use trusted images, protect metadata services, limit device access, apply secure boot or attestation where justified, and continuously assess drift.

## Expected evidence

- Hardening baselines, image records, configurations, compliance scans, drift findings, exceptions, and remediation.

## Assessment procedures

- **INF-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify baseline coverage for representative infrastructure components.
- **INF-110-A2 - Test:** Scan sampled components for baseline compliance and drift.
- **INF-110-A3 - Test:** Attempt access through prohibited services or management paths.
