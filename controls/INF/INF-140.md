# INF-140 AI Cryptographic Protection

## Requirement

The organization shall use approved cryptographic mechanisms and key management to protect AI data, models, credentials, administrative paths, communications, backups, and artifacts in transit and at rest according to classification and risk.

## Intent

Cryptography protects confidentiality and integrity across distributed AI platforms and supply chains.

## Applicability

Applies to organization-managed protection and configured responsibilities in external services.

## Implementation guidance

Define approved algorithms, protocols, certificates, key ownership, rotation, separation, revocation, backup, recovery, and cryptographic agility. Protect internal service and management traffic, not only public endpoints.

## Expected evidence

- Cryptographic standard, data-flow coverage, certificates, key inventory, rotation, configurations, tests, and exceptions.

## Assessment procedures

- **INF-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify cryptographic coverage against data classification and flows.
- **INF-140-A2 - Test:** Inspect representative transport, storage, backup, and management configurations.
- **INF-140-A3 - Test:** Validate key rotation, revocation, and recovery procedures.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Protected assets using approved cryptography | 100% | Continuous |
| Expired or unapproved keys and certificates in use | 0 | Continuous |
