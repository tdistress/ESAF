# INF-120 AI Infrastructure Vulnerability Management

## Requirement

The organization shall identify, assess, prioritize, remediate, mitigate, or accept vulnerabilities affecting AI infrastructure and dependencies according to exploitability, exposure, capability impact, data sensitivity, criticality, and available compensating controls.

## Intent

Generic severity alone may not reflect vulnerabilities that expose models, data, tools, or high-impact AI operations.

## Applicability

Applies to hosts, firmware, drivers, accelerators, containers, orchestration, model servers, libraries, storage, networks, and managed-service responsibilities.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain coverage and ownership, ingest supplier advisories, scan images and running assets, prioritize reachable and exploited conditions, test patches for model compatibility, and define emergency mitigation.

## Expected evidence

- Asset coverage, scans, advisories, prioritization, tickets, remediation, compensating controls, exceptions, and verification.

## Assessment procedures

- **INF-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify coverage, ownership, criteria, and service levels.
- **INF-120-A2 - Test:** Trace sampled findings through prioritization and verified disposition.
- **INF-120-A3 - Test:** Compare deployed components with vulnerability and advisory sources.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Critical exploitable findings beyond service level | 0 | Continuous |
| In-scope infrastructure with current assessment coverage | 100% | Monthly |
