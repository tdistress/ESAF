# INF-150 AI Capacity and Resource Safeguards

## Requirement

The organization shall establish and enforce capacity, quota, isolation, priority, budget, concurrency, scaling, and shutdown safeguards that prevent AI resource exhaustion, uncontrolled consumption, and harmful contention.

## Intent

AI compute and token consumption can create denial of service, cost shock, and interference between workloads.

## Applicability

Applies to compute, accelerators, memory, storage, network, model endpoints, tokens, tools, jobs, and shared platforms.

## Implementation guidance

Define hard and soft limits, priority for critical workloads, tenant quotas, abnormal-consumption alerts, circuit breakers, scaling ceilings, cost attribution, and recovery from exhausted resources.

## Expected evidence

- Capacity plan, quotas, budgets, isolation, scaling policy, alerts, load tests, cost reports, and incident records.

## Assessment procedures

- **INF-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify safeguards for all material constrained resources.
- **INF-150-A2 - Test:** Exercise quota, concurrency, budget, scaling, and shutdown limits.
- **INF-150-A3 - Test:** Simulate contention and verify priority and isolation behavior.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Consumption beyond approved hard limit | 0 | Continuous |
| Critical services meeting capacity objectives | Organization-defined | Monthly |
