# INF-100 Approved AI Hosting

## Requirement

The organization shall operate AI workloads, models, data, and supporting services only in hosting environments approved for their risk, classification, jurisdiction, architecture, isolation, assurance, and operational requirements.

## Intent

Hosting approval establishes the boundary in which infrastructure and inherited controls are trusted.

## Applicability

Applies to cloud, on-premises, edge, endpoint, laboratory, supplier, and hybrid environments.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain an approved environment catalog and deployment criteria. Address region, tenancy, data use, administration, logging, resilience, provider responsibility, accelerator sharing, and exit. Detect unauthorized hosting.

## Expected evidence

- Hosting standards, approved-environment records, architecture approval, deployment inventory, exceptions, and discovery results.

## Assessment procedures

- **INF-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify hosting approval against sampled workload risk and classification.
- **INF-100-A2 - Test:** Reconcile deployed AI resources with approved environments and inventory.
- **INF-100-A3 - Interview:** Confirm exception and unauthorized-hosting response.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| AI workloads in approved hosting | 100% | Continuous |
| Unauthorized hosting without disposition | 0 | Monthly |
