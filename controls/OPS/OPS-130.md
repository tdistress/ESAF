# OPS-130 AI Continuity and Recovery

## Requirement

The organization shall define, implement, and test risk-proportionate continuity, fallback, safe-state, recovery, dependency, data-restoration, and provider-failure arrangements for AI capabilities.

## Intent

AI service failure or degradation shall not create uncontrolled business, safety, rights, or operational impact.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4, with recovery rigor based on criticality, substitutability, and failure consequence.

## Implementation guidance

Define recovery objectives, manual or non-AI fallback, model and configuration restoration, provider alternatives, dependency sequencing, data consistency, degraded modes, and criteria for safe resumption.

## Expected evidence

- Business impact analysis, continuity and recovery plans, objectives, backups, dependency maps, tests, findings, and remediation.

## Assessment procedures

- **OPS-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify plans and objectives against risk and dependencies.
- **OPS-130-A2 - Test:** Exercise recovery, fallback, or safe-state operation.
- **OPS-130-A3 - Test:** Restore representative model, configuration, and required data.
