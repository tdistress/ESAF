# OPS-120 AI Incident Management

## Requirement

The organization shall prepare for, identify, triage, contain, investigate, eradicate, recover from, communicate, report, and learn from AI incidents using criteria and procedures integrated with enterprise incident management.

## Intent

AI incidents may involve model behavior, data, agents, suppliers, affected persons, and evidence not addressed by conventional playbooks.

## Applicability

Applies to security, privacy, safety, reliability, rights, compliance, misuse, model, data, agent, and supplier incidents.

## Implementation guidance

Define incident taxonomy and severity, model and data preservation, agent containment, provider coordination, affected-party and regulatory notification, rollback, redress, and exercise scenarios.

## Expected evidence

- AI playbooks, contacts, severity criteria, exercises, incident records, evidence, communications, recovery, and lessons learned.

## Assessment procedures

- **OPS-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify playbook coverage and integration with enterprise response.
- **OPS-120-A2 - Test:** Exercise an AI incident including containment and recovery.
- **OPS-120-A3 - Examine:** Trace sampled lessons learned to corrective action.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| High-risk AI incident exercises completed as scheduled | 100% | Annual |
| Material reporting deadlines missed | 0 | Per incident |
