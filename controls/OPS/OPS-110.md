# OPS-110 AI Change and Release Management

## Requirement

The organization shall classify, assess, test, approve, deploy, verify, communicate, and support rollback for changes to AI capabilities according to impact, risk, dependencies, and material-change triggers.

## Intent

AI behavior can change through data, models, prompts, tools, configuration, infrastructure, or suppliers.

## Applicability

Applies to planned, emergency, automated, supplier-driven, and rollback changes across all lifecycle environments.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain change records that link affected assets, risk, validation, approvals, deployment, monitoring, communications, and recovery. Define emergency review and distinguish routine from material change.

## Expected evidence

- Change criteria, records, testing, approvals, deployment, verification, rollback, communications, and emergency reviews.

## Assessment procedures

- **OPS-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify complete evidence for sampled changes.
- **OPS-110-A2 - Test:** Compare deployed state with approved release records.
- **OPS-110-A3 - Test:** Exercise rollback for a representative material change.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Unauthorized production changes | 0 | Continuous |
| Failed changes without timely recovery or disposition | 0 | Monthly |
