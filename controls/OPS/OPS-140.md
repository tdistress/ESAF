# OPS-140 AI Capacity and Performance Management

## Requirement

The organization shall monitor, forecast, and manage AI availability, latency, throughput, quality, capacity, utilization, demand, cost, and dependency performance against approved objectives.

## Intent

Operational performance includes response quality and cost as well as conventional service measures.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4 production services and shared AI platforms.

## Implementation guidance

Establish baselines, thresholds, seasonal and growth forecasts, quality indicators, token and compute attribution, supplier measures, capacity reservations, scaling, and corrective action.

## Expected evidence

- Objectives, dashboards, forecasts, utilization and cost reports, quality measures, alerts, reviews, and actions.

## Assessment procedures

- **OPS-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify coverage of required operational dimensions.
- **OPS-140-A2 - Test:** Recalculate sampled measures and compare source data.
- **OPS-140-A3 - Examine:** Trace threshold breaches to response and improvement.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Service objectives achieved | Organization-defined | Monthly |
| Unplanned capacity or cost breaches without disposition | 0 | Monthly |
