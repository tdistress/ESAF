# OPS-150 AI Capability Retirement

## Requirement

The organization shall retire an AI capability through an approved process that addresses users, dependencies, access, models, data, agents, interfaces, suppliers, contracts, records, communications, continuity, and verification of decommissioning.

## Intent

Capabilities can remain accessible or influential after nominal retirement through endpoints, data flows, integrations, and user workarounds.

## Applicability

Applies to experiments, pilots, production services, embedded capabilities, and third-party services.

## Implementation guidance

Plan transition, notification, archive, retention, deletion, credential revocation, endpoint removal, supplier exit, dependency update, inventory closure, and lessons learned. Verify no unauthorized residual operation.

## Expected evidence

- Retirement approval, impact analysis, communications, migration, disposal, access removal, supplier exit, inventory update, and verification.

## Assessment procedures

- **OPS-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify complete retirement evidence for sampled capabilities.
- **OPS-150-A2 - Test:** Attempt access through former endpoints, identities, integrations, and scheduled work.
- **OPS-150-A3 - Test:** Reconcile retired inventory state with active technical and financial records.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Retired capabilities retaining unauthorized access or operation | 0 | Monthly |
| Retirement actions completed by approved date | 100% | Monthly |
