# OPS-100 AI Service Ownership and Objectives

## Requirement

The organization shall assign each production AI capability an accountable service owner and approved objectives for availability, performance, quality, support, security, recovery, cost, monitoring, escalation, and maintenance.

## Intent

Production AI requires explicit operational accountability and measurable service expectations.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4 production capabilities and shared AI platforms.

## Implementation guidance

Define service boundaries, consumers, support model, dependencies, service levels, error budgets, maintenance windows, operational acceptance, and handoffs between business, engineering, platform, supplier, and security teams.

## Expected evidence

- Service record, owner, objectives, support model, dependencies, acceptance, dashboards, and review records.

## Assessment procedures

- **OPS-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify owner and approved objectives for sampled services.
- **OPS-100-A2 - Interview:** Confirm operational responsibilities and escalation paths.
- **OPS-100-A3 - Test:** Trace missed objectives to review and disposition.

## Metrics

| Material objective breaches without disposition | 0 | Monthly |
