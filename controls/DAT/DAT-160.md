# DAT-160 AI Output and Feedback Governance

## Requirement

The organization shall classify, validate, label, retain, share, reuse, and dispose of AI outputs and feedback according to their content, source data, intended use, reliability, intellectual-property status, privacy, and downstream impact.

## Intent

Generated content and user feedback can contain sensitive, inaccurate, protected, or newly derived information that requires governance.

## Applicability

Applies to responses, summaries, decisions, recommendations, generated code and media, labels, ratings, corrections, and feedback used for evaluation or training.

## Implementation guidance

Define when outputs require human review, source attribution, AI disclosure, confidence or limitation notices, security scanning, and retention. Prevent unapproved output from becoming authoritative data or training input. Treat feedback as potentially sensitive and adversarial.

## Expected evidence

- Output-handling rules, validation configurations, notices, review records, retention, feedback controls, and downstream-use approvals.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-160-A1 - Examine:** Verify handling rules for representative output and feedback types.
- **DAT-160-A2 - Test:** Trace sampled outputs into downstream systems and verify required validation and labeling.
- **DAT-160-A3 - Test:** Submit sensitive or adversarial feedback and verify handling safeguards.
