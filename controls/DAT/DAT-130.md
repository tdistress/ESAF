# DAT-130 AI Data Lineage and Provenance

## Requirement

The organization shall maintain traceable provenance and lineage for material AI data from source through acquisition, transformation, labeling, embedding, use, sharing, retention, and disposal.

## Intent

Lineage supports authorization, quality, reproducibility, impact analysis, incident response, rights handling, and retirement.

## Applicability

Applies to material data and derived representations. Granularity scales with risk, reproducibility needs, and obligations.

## Implementation guidance

Record source, owner, authority, time, version, transformations, tools, approvals, destinations, and relationships to models and capabilities. Use immutable identifiers and automated lineage where feasible.

## Expected evidence

- Lineage records, source manifests, transformation jobs, dataset versions, approval history, and AIBOM linkage.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify required lineage attributes for sampled material data.
- **DAT-130-A2 - Test:** Trace selected output or model data backward to authoritative sources.
- **DAT-130-A3 - Test:** Trace a source change forward to affected capabilities and models.
