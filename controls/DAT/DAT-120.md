# DAT-120 AI Data Quality and Suitability

## Requirement

The organization shall define, validate, and monitor risk-proportionate criteria for the accuracy, completeness, timeliness, relevance, representativeness, consistency, and fitness of data used by an AI capability.

## Intent

AI performance and impact depend on whether data is suitable for the approved purpose and operating context.

## Applicability

Applies to training, tuning, evaluation, retrieval, inference, feedback, and monitoring data; criteria vary by purpose and risk.

## Implementation guidance

Set thresholds and limitations before use. Assess missingness, imbalance, label quality, contamination, duplication, drift, and source reliability. Document unavoidable limitations and compensating safeguards.

## Expected evidence

- Quality criteria, profiles, validation results, exceptions, limitations, remediation, and ongoing monitoring.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify approved criteria and results for sampled datasets.
- **DAT-120-A2 - Test:** Recalculate selected quality measures from source data.
- **DAT-120-A3 - Examine:** Trace failed thresholds to remediation, restriction, or acceptance.
