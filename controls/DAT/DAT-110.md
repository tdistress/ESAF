# DAT-110 AI Data Classification and Handling

## Requirement

The organization shall classify AI data and enforce handling requirements for collection, access, transmission, storage, use, logging, sharing, retention, backup, archival, and disposal according to sensitivity and applicable obligations.

## Intent

AI introduces additional copies and derived representations that must inherit appropriate protection.

## Applicability

Applies to source data, prompts, responses, datasets, embeddings, vector stores, caches, logs, feedback, labels, and derived artifacts.

## Implementation guidance

Propagate classification metadata where feasible. Define approved services and model providers by classification. Apply encryption, masking, tokenization, minimization, access control, and retention based on handling rules.

## Expected evidence

- Classification records, handling standard, technical policies, configurations, transfer records, retention schedules, and disposal evidence.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify classification and handling rules for representative AI data types.
- **DAT-110-A2 - Test:** Trace selected data through storage, transmission, logs, backups, and disposal.
- **DAT-110-A3 - Test:** Attempt prohibited transfer to an unauthorized service.
