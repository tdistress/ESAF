# DAT-140 AI Privacy and Data Rights

## Requirement

The organization shall identify and implement privacy requirements for personal data processed by AI, including minimization, transparency, lawful basis, purpose limitation, retention, security, individual rights, automated-decision obligations, and cross-border transfer where applicable.

## Intent

AI processing can infer, reproduce, combine, or act on personal data in ways not evident from the original collection.

## Applicability

Applies when personal data is collected, inferred, retrieved, generated, logged, shared, or used to make or support decisions.

## Implementation guidance

Conduct privacy impact assessment before high-risk processing. Design mechanisms for access, correction, deletion, objection, appeal, and model or derived-data handling where required. Document technical limitations and alternative safeguards.

## Expected evidence

- Privacy assessment, processing record, notices, data-flow analysis, retention, rights procedures, transfer mechanisms, and request records.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify applicable privacy requirements and implemented measures.
- **DAT-140-A2 - Test:** Exercise a representative rights request through AI data and derived stores.
- **DAT-140-A3 - Examine:** Trace sampled personal-data processing to authority, notice, purpose, and retention.
