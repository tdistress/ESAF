# DAT-150 Retrieval, Embedding, and Vector Data Protection

## Requirement

The organization shall protect retrieval sources, embeddings, indexes, vector stores, and retrieved context with source authorization, classification-aware access, integrity, tenant isolation, retention, deletion, and monitoring controls equivalent to the underlying information risk.

## Intent

Embeddings and retrieval systems can expose protected information when source permissions and lifecycle controls are lost during indexing.

## Applicability

Applies to RAG, semantic search, long-term memory, recommendation, similarity, and other embedding-based systems.

## Implementation guidance

Preserve source identity, classification, owner, authorization, version, and deletion state. Enforce authorization before or during retrieval, not only after generation. Re-index when permissions or source content change and prevent cross-tenant retrieval.

## Expected evidence

- Retrieval architecture, metadata schema, access policies, source synchronization, deletion tests, isolation tests, and retrieval logs.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify that retrieval metadata and controls preserve source restrictions.
- **DAT-150-A2 - Test:** Attempt unauthorized and cross-tenant retrieval using representative identities.
- **DAT-150-A3 - Test:** Delete or restrict a source and verify timely removal from retrieval results.

## Metrics
