# DAT-100 AI Data Authority and Purpose

## Requirement

The organization shall document and approve the owner, lawful or contractual authority, permitted purpose, users, processing, sharing, retention, and prohibited uses for data consumed, generated, or materially transformed by an AI capability.

## Intent

Data should not enter an AI lifecycle without accountable authority and boundaries that persist through derived uses.

## Applicability

Applies to training, tuning, evaluation, retrieval, inference, prompt, output, feedback, telemetry, and derived data.

## Implementation guidance

Record purpose and authority before use. Address licenses, consent, confidentiality, intellectual property, localization, secondary use, and supplier terms. Reapprove expanded use or new affected populations as a material change.

## Expected evidence

- Data-owner approval, purpose record, authority, agreements, restrictions, retention, sharing, and change decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **DAT-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify authority and purpose for sampled material data sources and derived uses.
- **DAT-100-A2 - Test:** Compare observed processing and sharing with approved boundaries.
- **DAT-100-A3 - Interview:** Confirm how new purposes and sources receive approval.
