# RSK-140 Material Change Risk Review

## Requirement

The organization shall define material-change triggers and reassess, revalidate, and reauthorize an AI capability before implementing a change that could alter its purpose, users, affected parties, data, model, autonomy, architecture, supplier, obligations, performance, or control effectiveness.

## Intent

AI risk can change substantially through configuration, data, model, integration, or usage changes that appear operationally routine.

## Applicability

Applies to planned and externally imposed changes, including supplier model updates, changed terms, new tools, expanded data, new jurisdictions, increased autonomy, drift, incidents, and emergent use.

## Implementation guidance

Integrate triggers into change, model, data, procurement, architecture, and incident processes. Define emergency-change treatment and post-implementation review. Automate notification when suppliers or registered components change where feasible.

## Expected evidence

- Trigger criteria, change records, impact analysis, updated classification, validation, approvals, monitoring, and emergency-review records.

## Assessment procedures

- **RSK-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify that trigger criteria cover every required change category.
- **RSK-140-A2 - Test:** Trace selected material changes to reassessment, validation, and authorization.
- **RSK-140-A3 - Test:** Compare deployed versions and configurations with approved records to identify unreviewed change.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Material changes implemented without required authorization | 0 | Monthly |
| Emergency changes reviewed within defined period | 100% | Monthly |
