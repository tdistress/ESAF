# RSK-120 AI Risk Treatment and Acceptance

## Requirement

The organization shall document, approve, implement, and monitor treatments for identified AI risks and obtain acceptance of residual risk from an authority commensurate with the risk before production authorization or continued operation.

## Intent

Risk findings become controlled decisions with accountable ownership rather than unresolved observations.

## Applicability

Applies to risks requiring mitigation, avoidance, transfer, sharing, or acceptance. Treatment depth scales with risk and uncertainty.

## Implementation guidance

Each treatment plan should identify action, owner, due date, resources, dependencies, control linkage, target residual risk, evidence, and escalation. Acceptance should expire or be reviewed when assumptions, controls, impact, or appetite change.

## Expected evidence

- Risk register, treatment plans, control implementation evidence, residual-risk ratings, approvals, monitoring, and overdue-action escalation.

## Assessment procedures

- **RSK-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify that sampled material risks have complete treatment and acceptance records.
- **RSK-120-A2 - Test:** Confirm that treatment evidence supports the claimed residual risk.
- **RSK-120-A3 - Test:** Trace overdue or failed treatments to escalation and disposition.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Material residual risks accepted by authorized role | 100% | Monthly |
| Overdue high-priority treatments without escalation | 0 | Monthly |
