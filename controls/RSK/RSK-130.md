# RSK-130 AI Impact Assessment

## Requirement

The organization shall assess reasonably foreseeable beneficial and adverse impacts of E2 through E4 AI capabilities on the organization, individuals, affected groups, society, and the environment where relevant, and document evidence, uncertainty, stakeholder input, mitigations, and unresolved impacts before authorization.

## Intent

Traditional security and financial risk analysis may not identify effects on people, rights, access, fairness, safety, or the environment.

## Applicability

Required for E2 through E4. The assessment scope scales with affected parties, decision materiality, safety, rights, scale, and jurisdiction.

## Implementation guidance

Use multidisciplinary expertise. Consider intended and foreseeable misuse, differential effects, accessibility, contestability, feedback loops, cumulative impact, resource consumption, and groups underrepresented in development data. Engage affected stakeholders when appropriate and lawful.

## Expected evidence

- Impact assessment, evidence sources, assumptions, stakeholder input, limitations, mitigations, approvals, and monitoring commitments.

## Assessment procedures

- **RSK-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify coverage of applicable impact categories, evidence, uncertainty, and unresolved matters.
- **RSK-130-A2 - Interview:** Confirm participation by appropriate domain and affected-party expertise.
- **RSK-130-A3 - Test:** Trace identified material impacts to treatment, disclosure, monitoring, or acceptance.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Applicable capabilities with approved impact assessment | 100% | Each authorization |
| Material impacts lacking treatment or authorized acceptance | 0 | Quarterly |
