# RSK-100 Enterprise AI Risk Methodology

## Requirement

The organization shall maintain and consistently apply an approved AI risk methodology that defines scope, risk dimensions, rating criteria, evidence, inherent and residual risk, aggregation, treatment, acceptance, monitoring, reassessment, and escalation.

## Intent

A common method produces comparable decisions across capabilities and connects AI risk to enterprise risk governance.

## Applicability

Applies to all in-scope capabilities. Assessment depth and evidence scale with baseline and uncertainty.

## Implementation guidance

Integrate with enterprise risk management while addressing AI-specific uncertainty, data and model limitations, affected parties, autonomy, misuse, supply chain, concentration, and cumulative impact. Define qualitative or quantitative rating criteria and risk appetite thresholds.

## Expected evidence

- Approved methodology, rating scales, risk dimensions, appetite thresholds, templates, guidance, and completed assessments.
- Calibration or quality-review records demonstrating consistent application.

## Assessment procedures

- **RSK-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify that the method defines all required elements.
- **RSK-100-A2 - Test:** Compare ratings across similar capabilities for consistent application.
- **RSK-100-A3 - Interview:** Confirm how uncertainty, aggregation, and risk appetite influence decisions.
