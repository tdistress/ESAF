# RSK-110 AI Capability Risk Classification

## Requirement

The organization shall classify each in-scope AI capability before its approved use using documented criteria for impact, likelihood, uncertainty, autonomy, data sensitivity, affected parties, external exposure, criticality, reversibility, dependencies, and applicable obligations.

## Intent

Classification determines proportionate lifecycle gates, controls, evidence, oversight, monitoring, assurance, and acceptance authority.

## Applicability

Applies when a capability enters the inventory and whenever a material trigger could change its classification.

## Implementation guidance

Record both capability tier and risk rating. Define conservative handling when information is incomplete. Classification may consider the highest-impact credible use or failure rather than average use alone.

## Expected evidence

- Classification criteria, completed classification record, rationale, reviewers, approval, and baseline selection.

## Assessment procedures

- **RSK-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify that sampled classifications address every required factor.
- **RSK-110-A2 - Test:** Independently apply the criteria to a sample and compare the result.
- **RSK-110-A3 - Test:** Identify capabilities lacking classification or operating above the approved scope.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| In-scope capabilities with current classification | 100% | Monthly |
| Classification overrides without documented approval | 0 | Monthly |
