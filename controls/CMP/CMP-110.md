# CMP-110 AI Records, Notices, and Reporting

## Requirement

The organization shall create, protect, retain, disclose, submit, and dispose of AI records, notices, registrations, reports, and evidence according to applicable content, timing, audience, accessibility, integrity, confidentiality, and retention requirements.

## Intent

Compliance depends on accurate and timely records and communications, not solely technical control operation.

## Applicability

Applies where obligations require internal or external documentation, transparency, registration, notification, or reporting.

## Implementation guidance

Define responsible role, trigger, template, review, approval, delivery evidence, correction, legal hold, and retention. Coordinate public claims with technical evidence and incident reporting.

## Expected evidence

- Record schedules, notices, reports, approvals, submission receipts, disclosures, corrections, and disposal evidence.

## Assessment procedures

- **CMP-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify sampled records and notices against applicable requirements.
- **CMP-110-A2 - Test:** Trace triggering events to timely reporting and delivery evidence.
