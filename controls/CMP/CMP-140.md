# CMP-140 AI Intellectual Property and Licensing

## Requirement

The organization shall identify, approve, comply with, and retain evidence of intellectual-property ownership, license, attribution, usage, distribution, training, output, indemnity, and restriction requirements for AI data, models, code, prompts, tools, and generated content.

## Intent

AI supply chains combine licensed and generated assets whose rights and restrictions may conflict with intended use.

## Applicability

Applies to acquired, open, commercial, internal, contributed, scraped, generated, and modified assets.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain license and rights records, approved-use criteria, attribution, source review, output terms, open-source obligations, restrictions on training and redistribution, and legal review for ambiguity.

## Expected evidence

- Rights inventory, licenses, terms, approvals, attribution, source records, reviews, restrictions, and disposition.

## Assessment procedures

- **CMP-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify rights and restrictions for sampled material assets and uses.
- **CMP-140-A2 - Test:** Trace distributed or reused outputs to required review and attribution.
