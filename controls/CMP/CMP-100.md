# CMP-100 AI Obligation Management

## Requirement

The organization shall identify, interpret, record, assign, implement, monitor, and update legal, regulatory, contractual, policy, ethical, and sector obligations applicable to each AI capability and the enterprise AI management system.

## Intent

Obligations vary by jurisdiction, data, affected parties, sector, role, purpose, and risk and shall be translated into accountable requirements.

## Applicability

Applies to all capabilities and enterprise governance, with depth based on scope and applicable authority.

## Implementation guidance

Maintain a versioned obligation register linked to capabilities, owners, controls, evidence, effective dates, interpretations, and monitoring. Obtain qualified legal advice for material ambiguity.

## Expected evidence

- Obligation register, legal interpretations, applicability decisions, owners, control links, changes, and compliance status.

## Assessment procedures

- **CMP-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify completeness and currency of sampled obligations and applicability decisions.
- **CMP-100-A2 - Test:** Trace obligations to implemented controls and retained evidence.
