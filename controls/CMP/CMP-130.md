# CMP-130 AI Jurisdiction, Residency, and Transfer

## Requirement

The organization shall identify and enforce jurisdiction, residency, localization, transfer, government-access, and geographic-support requirements for AI data, models, telemetry, personnel, suppliers, and processing locations.

## Intent

Distributed AI services can move data and processing across regions and legal regimes without visible application change.

## Applicability

Applies where geography affects law, contract, policy, sovereignty, security, or customer commitment.

## Implementation guidance

Map processing and support locations, configure permitted regions, control failover and backups, assess transfer mechanisms and remote access, require supplier notice, and monitor location drift.

## Expected evidence

- Geographic data flows, residency decisions, transfer mechanisms, provider regions, configuration, access, and monitoring.

## Assessment procedures

- **CMP-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify geographic requirements and approved locations.
- **CMP-130-A2 - Test:** Compare actual processing, storage, support, and failover locations with approvals.
