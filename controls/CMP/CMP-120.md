# CMP-120 Third-Party AI Compliance

## Requirement

The organization shall establish, contractually assign, verify, monitor, and enforce third-party AI obligations for security, privacy, data use, intellectual property, transparency, change, incidents, assurance, continuity, subcontractors, and exit.

## Intent

Outsourcing AI services does not transfer organizational accountability for applicable obligations.

## Applicability

Applies to AI providers, model hosts, SaaS, data and evaluation services, tools, plugins, consultants, and subprocessors.

## Implementation guidance

Use due diligence, shared-responsibility matrices, minimum terms, evidence rights, change and incident notice, flow-down requirements, remedies, audit rights, and termination assistance.

## Expected evidence

- Assessments, contracts, responsibility matrices, assurance, monitoring, notices, findings, remediation, and exit records.

## Assessment procedures

- **CMP-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify required terms and responsibilities for sampled suppliers.
- **CMP-120-A2 - Test:** Trace supplier evidence and changes to review and enforcement.
