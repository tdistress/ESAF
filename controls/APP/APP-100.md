# APP-100 Secure AI Application Design

## Requirement

The organization shall design each AI application using documented trust boundaries, data and control flows, threat and misuse cases, security requirements, failure modes, human-oversight points, and risk-proportionate validation criteria.

## Intent

AI-specific threats and failures must be addressed in architecture rather than added after implementation.

## Applicability

Applies to internally developed, configured, embedded, customer-facing, and third-party-integrated AI applications.

## Implementation guidance

Address untrusted content, prompt injection, data leakage, excessive agency, insecure output use, model and supplier dependencies, cross-tenant exposure, resource abuse, fallback, and monitoring.

## Expected evidence

- Architecture, data-flow and trust-boundary diagrams, threat model, misuse cases, requirements, and design-review decisions.

## Assessment procedures

- **APP-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify that design evidence covers required AI-specific topics.
- **APP-100-A2 - Interview:** Confirm that identified threats trace to requirements and owners.
- **APP-100-A3 - Test:** Trace selected misuse cases to implemented and validated safeguards.
