# APP-150 AI Abuse and Resource Protection

## Requirement

The organization shall detect, constrain, and respond to AI application misuse, adversarial behavior, automated abuse, denial of service, excessive consumption, repeated policy evasion, and use outside approved purpose.

## Intent

AI endpoints can amplify cost, unsafe content, data extraction, model probing, and automated attack at scale.

## Applicability

Applies to internal and external interfaces, including public services, batch jobs, agents, and privileged workflows.

## Implementation guidance

Use quotas, rate and concurrency limits, budgets, anomaly detection, abuse signals, progressive friction, account and device controls, content policy, circuit breakers, and coordinated incident response.

## Expected evidence

- Abuse cases, thresholds, limits, monitoring rules, alerts, response procedures, test results, and incident records.

## Assessment procedures

- **APP-150-A1 - Examine:** Verify protections for identified abuse and resource scenarios.
- **APP-150-A2 - Test:** Exercise rate, quota, budget, and concurrency safeguards.
- **APP-150-A3 - Test:** Simulate repeated policy evasion and verify detection and response.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Resource consumption exceeding approved hard limits | 0 | Continuous |
| High-confidence abuse alerts handled within service level | 100% | Monthly |
