# APP-120 Output Validation and Safe Consumption

## Requirement

The organization shall validate, constrain, encode, label, and authorize AI output before presentation, execution, persistence, or downstream use according to content, destination, intended action, uncertainty, and impact.

## Intent

AI output is untrusted data and may be inaccurate, sensitive, malicious, malformed, or unsafe for downstream systems.

## Applicability

Applies to human-facing content, code, queries, commands, structured data, decisions, recommendations, tool parameters, and machine-consumed output.

## Implementation guidance

Use schemas, allowlists, contextual encoding, content and data-loss checks, source attribution, human review, confidence or limitation notices, sandboxing, and target-system authorization. Avoid direct execution of free-form output.

## Expected evidence

- Output contracts, validation rules, encoding, approval logic, downstream authorization, tests, and failure handling.

## Assessment procedures

- **APP-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify validation and authorization for representative output destinations.
- **APP-120-A2 - Test:** Generate malformed, sensitive, and adversarial output and observe handling.
- **APP-120-A3 - Test:** Attempt unauthorized execution or persistence through model output.
