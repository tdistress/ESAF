# GOV-120 AI Portfolio Oversight

## Requirement

The organization shall review the enterprise AI portfolio at an organization-defined frequency using current information on ownership, lifecycle state, value, risk, incidents, exceptions, obligations, dependencies, concentration, cost, and retirement.

## Intent

Portfolio oversight enables leadership to prioritize investment, identify aggregated risk, reduce duplication, and retire capabilities that no longer justify their exposure or cost.

## Applicability

Applies to the aggregated set of production, pilot, embedded, and material third-party AI capabilities. E0 experiments may be reported through aggregated innovation governance.

## Implementation guidance

Use the authoritative inventory as the portfolio source. Establish thresholds for executive escalation and views by business function, tier, supplier, model, data classification, jurisdiction, and lifecycle state.

## Organization-defined parameters

- `[portfolio review frequency]`: no less frequently than annually; E3 and E4 portfolio risk should be reviewed more frequently.

## Expected evidence

- Portfolio dashboard, review agenda, decisions, actions, and escalation records.
- Reconciliation between portfolio reporting and the authoritative AI inventory.

## Assessment procedures

- **GOV-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify that portfolio reporting contains the required information and follows the defined frequency.
- **GOV-120-A2 - Test:** Trace selected portfolio decisions to supporting capability records and completed actions.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio records current by review date | 100% | Each review |
| Overdue portfolio actions | Organization-defined threshold | Monthly |
