# GOV-110 Enterprise AI Policy

## Requirement

The organization shall maintain an approved enterprise AI policy that defines scope, principles, acceptable and prohibited use, lifecycle obligations, accountability, risk expectations, exception handling, enforcement, and review requirements.

## Intent

A single authoritative policy establishes consistent expectations while supporting risk-based implementation through standards and procedures.

## Applicability

Applies to personnel, contractors, relevant third parties, and all in-scope AI capabilities.

## Implementation guidance

Align the policy hierarchy with existing enterprise governance. Communicate requirements through onboarding, training, approved-service catalogs, technical enforcement, and periodic acknowledgment where appropriate. Review after material legal, technology, incident, or organizational change.

## Expected evidence

- Approved, version-controlled policy with owner and review date.
- Communication, acknowledgment, exception, enforcement, and review records.

## Assessment procedures

- **GOV-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify that all required policy topics are addressed and approved.
- **GOV-110-A2 - Interview:** Confirm that responsible roles understand policy ownership and enforcement.
- **GOV-110-A3 - Test:** Trace selected AI uses and exceptions to applicable policy requirements.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Policy reviewed by due date | 100% | Annual and event-driven |
| In-scope workforce receiving required policy communication | Organization-defined | Quarterly |
