# GOV-130 AI Capability Accountability

## Requirement

The organization shall assign each in-scope AI capability an accountable business owner and technical or service owner with documented authority for purpose, outcomes, risk, resources, operation, material change, and retirement.

## Intent

Named ownership prevents gaps between business use, technical operation, and risk acceptance.

## Applicability

Applies from ideation through retirement. An individual may hold both roles when conflicts are evaluated and oversight remains appropriate.

## Implementation guidance

Record owners in the authoritative inventory. Define deputies, transfer procedures, and escalation when ownership is vacant. Assign supporting security, privacy, data, legal, compliance, operational, and assurance responsibilities according to risk.

## Expected evidence

- Inventory records, role descriptions, acceptance records, and ownership-transfer history.
- Decisions demonstrating owner participation at lifecycle gates.

## Assessment procedures

- **GOV-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify that sampled capabilities have current owners and documented authority.
- **GOV-130-A2 - Interview:** Confirm sampled owners understand and exercise their responsibilities.
- **GOV-130-A3 - Test:** Identify capabilities with missing or inactive owners and verify escalation.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| In-scope capabilities with current business and technical owners | 100% | Monthly |
| Ownership vacancies exceeding threshold | 0 | Monthly |
