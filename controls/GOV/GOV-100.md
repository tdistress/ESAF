# GOV-100 Enterprise AI Governance Authority

## Requirement

Executive leadership shall establish and maintain an enterprise authority with documented accountability, membership, decision rights, escalation paths, and meeting cadence for governing in-scope AI capabilities.

## Intent

AI decisions require an identifiable authority that can resolve cross-functional issues and accept accountability for enterprise outcomes.

## Applicability

Applies to the enterprise AI management system and every baseline. Existing governance bodies may satisfy the requirement when AI responsibilities are explicit.

## Implementation guidance

Document the authority in a charter. Include business, technology, security, privacy, data, legal, risk, compliance, operations, and assurance participation appropriate to scope. Define which decisions are delegated and which require executive or board escalation.

## Expected evidence

- Approved charter, membership, delegated authorities, meeting records, and decision log.
- Evidence that material risks and unresolved matters are escalated.

## Assessment procedures

- **GOV-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify that the charter defines accountability, membership, decision rights, escalation, and cadence.
- **GOV-100-A2 - Interview:** Confirm that members understand their authority and exercise it in practice.
- **GOV-100-A3 - Test:** Trace selected material decisions to the authorized body and retained evidence.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Required governance meetings held | 100% of scheduled meetings | Quarterly |
| Material decisions with authorized disposition | 100% | Quarterly |
