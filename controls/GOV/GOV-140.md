# GOV-140 AI Exception Governance

## Requirement

The organization shall authorize ESAF exceptions only through a documented, time-bounded process that identifies scope, justification, risk, compensating measures, accountable owner, approval authority, monitoring, remediation, expiration, and review conditions.

## Intent

Exceptions make temporary deviations visible, risk-owned, monitored, and reversible rather than becoming permanent undocumented practice.

## Applicability

Applies to deviations from ESAF requirements, selected controls, baselines, profiles, and internal AI standards. Exceptions cannot waive applicable law or external contractual obligations.

## Implementation guidance

Set approval authority according to residual risk. Maintain exceptions in a central register. Notify affected control and service owners, monitor compensating measures, and automatically escalate upcoming or expired exceptions.

## Expected evidence

- Exception procedure and register.
- Approved requests, risk decisions, compensating-control evidence, monitoring, and closure records.

## Assessment procedures

- **GOV-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify that the process and sampled exceptions contain every required element.
- **GOV-140-A2 - Test:** Identify expired exceptions and verify closure, renewal, or escalation.
- **GOV-140-A3 - Test:** Confirm that compensating measures operated during the exception period.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Expired exceptions without authorized disposition | 0 | Monthly |
| Exceptions with verified compensating measures | 100% | Monthly |
