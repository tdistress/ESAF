# EDU-130 Secure AI Development Training

## Requirement

The organization shall require current secure AI engineering training for personnel who design, build, configure, test, deploy, or materially maintain AI applications, models, data pipelines, platforms, agents, tools, and integrations.

## Intent

AI engineering introduces risks not fully addressed by conventional secure-development training.

## Applicability

Applies before unsupervised work and periodically thereafter according to role and technology change.

## Implementation guidance

Cover threat modeling, prompt and context attacks, output handling, RAG authorization, model and data supply chain, agents, MCP, secrets, testing, monitoring, privacy, and incident response.

## Expected evidence

- Role mapping, curriculum, completion, practical assessment, refresh, exceptions, and remediation.

## Assessment procedures

- **EDU-130-A1 - Examine:** Verify role-specific coverage and currency.
- **EDU-130-A2 - Test:** Sample assigned engineers for completion and practical competence.
