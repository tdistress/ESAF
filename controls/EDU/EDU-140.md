# EDU-140 AI Governance and Executive Education

## Requirement

The organization shall provide role-specific education to executives, governing authorities, accountable owners, risk acceptors, reviewers, and auditors on AI strategy, duties, risk, evidence, limitations, incidents, obligations, and decision rights.

## Intent

Governance decisions require informed challenge and cannot be delegated entirely to technical specialists.

## Applicability

Applies before exercising material authority and at a frequency reflecting risk and change.

## Implementation guidance

Use portfolio and scenario exercises, incident lessons, regulatory updates, risk-appetite decisions, conformance boundaries, and role-specific case studies.

## Expected evidence

- Audience mapping, curriculum, participation, exercises, comprehension, updates, and remediation.

## Assessment procedures

- **EDU-140-A1 - Examine:** Verify education against assigned governance duties.
- **EDU-140-A2 - Interview:** Confirm sampled leaders understand authority, evidence, and escalation.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Material decision-makers completing current education | 100% | Annual |
