# EDU-100 Enterprise AI Literacy

## Requirement

The organization shall provide and periodically refresh baseline AI literacy for personnel whose work can affect or be affected by AI, covering capabilities, limitations, accountability, safe use, data protection, verification, bias, escalation, and applicable policy.

## Intent

Everyday AI decisions require enough understanding to avoid misplaced trust, unsafe disclosure, and unreported risk.

## Applicability

Applies to employees, contractors, and relevant third parties according to role and access.

## Implementation guidance

Use role-relevant examples, accessible formats, onboarding, periodic refresh, change-triggered updates, and comprehension checks rather than attendance alone.

## Expected evidence

- Curriculum, audience, assignment, completion, comprehension, refresh, accessibility, and remediation records.

## Assessment procedures

- **EDU-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify curriculum coverage and assignment population.
- **EDU-100-A2 - Test:** Sample comprehension and completion evidence.
