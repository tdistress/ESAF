# EDU-110 Role-Based AI Competency

## Requirement

The organization shall define, assess, maintain, and evidence AI competency requirements for roles that design, develop, validate, approve, operate, monitor, secure, govern, audit, or materially use AI capabilities.

## Intent

Specialized responsibilities require demonstrated competence beyond general awareness.

## Applicability

Applies to internal and external personnel assigned material AI responsibilities.

## Implementation guidance

Define knowledge, skills, experience, supervision, continuing education, and requalification by role. Address gaps through training, mentoring, reassignment, or qualified external support.

## Expected evidence

- Competency framework, role profiles, assessments, qualifications, development plans, supervision, and reassessment.

## Assessment procedures

- **EDU-110-A1 - Examine:** Verify competency criteria for sampled material roles.
- **EDU-110-A2 - Test:** Compare assigned personnel evidence with role requirements.

## Metrics

| Metric | Target | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Material AI roles staffed by personnel meeting competency criteria | 100% | Quarterly |
