# EDU-120 AI Acceptable-Use Education

## Requirement

The organization shall educate AI users on approved services, permitted and prohibited uses, data-handling boundaries, output verification, human accountability, intellectual property, privacy, security, incident reporting, and exception processes before access and after material change.

## Intent

Acceptable-use requirements must be understandable at the moment users select tools, enter data, and rely on output.

## Applicability

Applies to personnel and third parties receiving access to enterprise AI services.

## Implementation guidance

Combine training with contextual notices, approved-tool catalogs, examples by data classification, attestations where justified, and targeted remediation after misuse.

## Expected evidence

- Materials, assignment, completion, acknowledgment, contextual notices, updates, and remediation records.

## Assessment procedures

- **EDU-120-A1 - Examine:** Verify required topics and timing for sampled user groups.
- **EDU-120-A2 - Test:** Confirm that access and material changes trigger required education.
