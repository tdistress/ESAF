# API-100 Enterprise AI Gateway

## Requirement

The organization shall route production AI service access through approved policy-enforcement points that provide identity, authorization, model and provider routing, data controls, logging, limits, policy enforcement, and emergency restriction appropriate to risk.

## Intent

Consistent control points reduce unmanaged direct integrations and provide enterprise visibility and response capability.

## Applicability

Applies to E1 through E4 production access to shared, external, or centrally managed AI services. Document exceptions for architectures that enforce equivalent controls elsewhere.

## Implementation guidance

Separate management and data planes, avoid a single unresilient dependency, preserve end-user identity, enforce classification and routing policy, support provider isolation, and define bypass detection.

## Expected evidence

- Gateway architecture, routing and policy configuration, identity propagation, logs, resilience, bypass monitoring, and exceptions.

## Assessment procedures

- **API-100-A1 - Examine:** Verify required policy-enforcement capabilities and approved routing.
- **API-100-A2 - Test:** Attempt unapproved direct provider access and verify detection or prevention.
- **API-100-A3 - Test:** Exercise emergency provider or model restriction.
