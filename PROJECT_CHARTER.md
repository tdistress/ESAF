**Document ID:** ESAF-0000  
**Status:** Working Draft  
**Version:** 0.1-alpha  
**Owner:** ESAF Project Maintainers  

---

## 1. Purpose

The Enterprise Secure AI Framework (ESAF) project exists to develop a vendor-neutral enterprise standard for the secure governance, architecture, operation, and assurance of Artificial Intelligence.

ESAF is intended to help organizations adopt AI responsibly by integrating governance, cybersecurity, enterprise architecture, risk management, data governance, operations, compliance, and continuous improvement into a coherent operating model.

---

## 2. Vision

To become the definitive vendor-neutral enterprise standard for governing, securing, architecting, operating, and assuring Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 3. Mission

Enable organizations to adopt AI responsibly by providing a practical, implementation-focused framework that integrates governance, security, architecture, operations, compliance, and continuous improvement.

---

## 4. Scope

ESAF applies to enterprise use of:

- Generative AI
- Large Language Models
- Machine Learning systems
- Predictive AI
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Enterprise copilots
- Agentic AI
- AI-enabled SaaS platforms
- AI-assisted software development
- Internally developed AI platforms
- Third-party AI services

## 6. Success Criteria

ESAF succeeds when organizations can use it to:

- Inventory and classify AI capabilities.
- Govern AI throughout its lifecycle.
- Apply risk-based controls.
- Build secure reference architectures.
- Map AI controls to external standards.
- Assess AI maturity.
- Demonstrate audit-ready evidence.
