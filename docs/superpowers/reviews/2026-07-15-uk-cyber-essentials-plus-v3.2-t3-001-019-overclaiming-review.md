## T3-014 and AUD-120 determination

`CEPTS3.2-T3-014 -> AUD-120` is a justified `partially_supports`, `narrow`,
high-confidence relationship. The external clause requires the Assessor to
note the result after observing attempted attachment openings. The exact
pinned `AUD-120` requirement independently requires the organization to obtain
and retain relevant, reliable, complete, timely, attributable, and
integrity-protected evidence sufficient to support each AI assessment
procedure and determination. That normative evidence-acquisition and
retention duty directly contributes to recording an attachment-opening
outcome when the attempt is governed as an AI assessment procedure.

The condition narrows the leg to an attachment-opening attempt governed under
ESAF whose outcome is evidence for the related determination. It does not
create or require the Cyber Essentials Plus procedure, assign it to an
Assessor, establish that an attempt occurred, supply an observed outcome, or
extend the relationship to every selected attachment. The rationale and
expected evidence claim only an attributable, integrity-protected link between
one governed attempt and its outcome. The known gaps expressly preserve the
absent scheme procedure, actor assignment, and per-attachment population rule;
the prohibited inferences preserve the absent execution, observed blocking,
complete population, test success, and certification outcomes. Consequently,
the relationship exists from the exact normative evidence duty without the
condition manufacturing a missing procedure or per-attempt result, while the
uncovered parts remain outside the leg.
