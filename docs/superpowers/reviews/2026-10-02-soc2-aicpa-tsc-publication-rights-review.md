## Evidence boundary

The AICPA resource page identifies the publication as the 2017 Trust Services
Criteria with revised 2022 Points of Focus and describes criteria for
Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy.
The page says the PDF is available with a free account and labels the file as
including copyright information. This establishes the public source identity
and access path, but not the content or terms of the downloadable PDF.

The observed AICPA site Terms & Conditions describe website content as
protected and restrict copying, modification, distribution, public use, and
derivative works absent written consent. They also object to text/data mining
and scraping of the site. The relationship between those website terms and
the separately downloadable Trust Services Criteria, including any
document-specific license or notice, has not been verified. The licensing
page identifies an AICPA licensing route, but no case-specific permission for
ESAF is evidenced.

Accordingly, free-account availability is not treated as permission to
republish criteria content or distribute an ESAF mapping. No document bytes,
checksum, page count, provision count, or source-derived inventory were
obtained. The review does not decide the availability or scope of any
statutory exception.

does not assert permission to copy, transform, distribute, or analyze the
criteria, and it does not establish a license for any field class above.

## Reconsideration trigger

Reconsideration requires an authorized source custodian to obtain the exact
English source and its document-specific copyright notice or license, plus
written AICPA permission if needed for the proposed publication. An
independent publication-rights reviewer must verify permission for every
proposed field class, repository and downstream publication channel, and
redistribution model before any criterion-level inventory or mapping analysis
is authored. Any permission must be attributable and specify required notices,
attribution, version limits, and restrictions.

## Decision boundaries

This review is not legal advice, does not interpret the AICPA terms as a final
legal opinion about the downloadable criteria, and does not determine whether
any statutory exception applies. It records that affirmative permission for
the proposed public ESAF use is not evidenced. It does not claim AICPA
approval, authorization, validation, endorsement, equivalence, certification,
SOC 2 compliance, assurance, or legal sufficiency. It does not create a
criterion inventory or mapping artifact.

## Final decision

`HOLD`. No source-derived provision inventory, paraphrase, or derivative
mapping analysis may be published under this readiness package until the
reconsideration evidence is independently reviewed and the mechanical matrix
derives a new decision.
