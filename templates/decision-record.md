# Decision Record Template

**Status:** Draft starter  
**Class:** Decision  
**Informative for:** ESAF-1000 Appendix A architecture decision record;
ESAF-1300 decision rights; ESAF-1400 mapping practice.

This non-normative template helps operators record a governed decision such as
an architecture choice, gate outcome, or production authorization input. It
does not create new decision rights.
