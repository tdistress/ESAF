# Risk Assessment Template

**Status:** Draft starter  
**Class:** Risk  
**Informative for:** ESAF-1000 Appendix A AI risk assessment; ESAF-1300 and
ESAF-1400 operating practice.

This non-normative template helps operators record inherent risk, treatment,
and residual risk for an AI capability. It does not redefine ESAF risk tiers or
control baselines.
