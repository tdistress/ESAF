# Retirement Record Template

**Status:** Draft starter  
**Class:** Retirement  
**Informative for:** ESAF-1000 Appendix A retirement record; ESAF-1300
retirement gate; ESAF-1400 optimize-phase retirement practice.

This non-normative template helps operators record decommissioning, access
removal, and data disposition for an AI capability. It does not redefine
retirement-gate evidence requirements.
